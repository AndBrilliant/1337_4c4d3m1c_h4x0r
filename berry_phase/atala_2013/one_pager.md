# Atala et al. 2013 — Direct measurement of the Zak phase

## Reference
M. Atala, M. Aidelsburger, J. T. Barreiro, D. Abanin, T. Kitagawa, E. Demler, I. Bloch, *Direct measurement of the Zak phase in topological Bloch bands*, **Nat. Phys. 9 (2013) 795–800**; arXiv:1212.0572.

## Source
§2 (Berry phase), tier ✦ — experimental measurement of a $\pi$ Zak phase in an SSH-type cold-atom chain.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Reports the first direct experimental measurement of the Zak phase using cold ⁸⁷Rb atoms in an optical superlattice realizing the dimerized (SSH) tight-binding chain. By performing a Stückelberg-interferometry protocol with Bloch oscillations through the Brillouin zone, the experimenters extract a Zak phase of $\phi_{\rm Zak} = \pi$ in the topologically non-trivial dimerized configuration, and $0$ in the trivial one. The two configurations differ only in the relative phase of the two dimerization patterns; the topological transition between them is a phase transition signaled by a $\pi$ jump in the Zak phase.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Best experimental example of a quantized Berry phase fixing a measurable (rather than just inferred) spectral parameter. Cited together with Jauregui et al. 2015 and Zak 1989 to establish the CM experimental tradition that supports Shulga's theoretical derivation of $\delta = 2/9$.
