# Jauregui et al. 2015 — Berry's phase fixes a Dirac mass in Bi₂Te₃

## Reference
L. A. Jauregui, M. T. Pettes, L. P. Rokhinson, M. Shi, Y. P. Chen, *Gate-tunable relativistic mass and Berry's phase in topological insulator nanoribbon field-effect devices*, **Scientific Reports 5 (2015) 8452**.

## Source
§2 (Berry phase), tier ★ — experimental example of "Berry phase fixes a Dirac mass."

## Beyond-abstract summary
Reports gate-tunable observation of the relativistic Dirac mass and the Berry phase in Bi₂Te₃ topological-insulator nanoribbons via Aharonov–Bohm magnetoresistance. The $\pi$ Berry phase of the topological surface states is directly read off from the magnetoresistance oscillations, and a gate-tunable effective Dirac mass (from $\sim 0$ to several meV) is extracted from the Landau-level spectrum. This is the cleanest experimental demonstration that a Berry phase fixes (or tunes) a mass-like spectral parameter in a CM system.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Concrete experimental analog of "Berry phase ↔ mass parameter" in the spirit of Shulga's $\delta = 2/9$ derivation. Cited when a referee asks for an experimental rather than theoretical CM precedent.
