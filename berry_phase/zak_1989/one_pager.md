# Zak 1989 — Berry's phase for energy bands in solids

## Reference
J. Zak, *Berry's phase for energy bands in solids*, **Phys. Rev. Lett. 62 (1989) 2747–2750**. (No arXiv; pre-arXiv era.)

## Beyond-abstract summary
Defines the "Zak phase" — the Berry phase accumulated by a Bloch state as the crystal momentum traverses the 1D Brillouin zone. For a 1D crystal with inversion symmetry, the Zak phase is quantized to 0 or $\pi$ (mod $2\pi$), making it the canonical $Z_2$-valued Berry phase. The Zak phase is directly related to the electric polarization of an insulator (the modern theory of polarization, formalized later by King-Smith and Vanderbilt). The construction is the foundation of all subsequent 1D topological-classification work (SSH model, polarization, edge states).

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Foundational reference for the position that a Berry phase can be quantized to a discrete rational value — establishing the CM precedent for Shulga's $\delta = 2/9$ as a "Berry-quantized" parameter. Note (per the research report caveat) that the Zak phase quantization gives $0$ or $\pi$, not a non-quantized rational like $2/9$ — Shulga's mechanism is therefore a *novel extension* of the Zak / TKNN / Atala pattern, not a direct instance.
