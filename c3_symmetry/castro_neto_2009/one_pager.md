# Castro Neto, Guinea, Peres, Novoselov & Geim 2009 — Electronic properties of graphene (RMP)

## Reference
A. H. Castro Neto, F. Guinea, N. M. R. Peres, K. S. Novoselov, A. K. Geim, *The electronic properties of graphene*, **Rev. Mod. Phys. 81 (2009) 109–162**; arXiv:0709.1163.

## Source
§3 (C₃ symmetry in CM), tier ★ — the canonical reference for the honeycomb $C_{3v}$ point group protecting Dirac points and producing trigonal warping.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The standard graphene RMP review. Foundational for "$C_3$ in CM": the honeycomb-lattice point group $C_{6v}$ contains $C_3$ as its rotation subgroup; $C_3$ protects the Dirac points at $K$ and $K'$ and constrains the sublattice mass term. Trigonal warping of the Fermi surface and Dirac-cone dispersion is a direct $C_3$ signature, observed experimentally in ARPES on graphene. Covers single-layer / bilayer / multilayer; magnetic-field response; disorder and electron–electron interactions; chemical functionalization. Cited several thousand times.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Canonical CM reference establishing that $C_3$ is a routine symmetry-protection mechanism, not an exotic one. Cited (with Ortix et al. 2012) when establishing the CM provenance of the $C_3$ factor in the F-identity construction's $C_3 \times Z_2$ symmetry.
