> §1 of the source report: *Descartes circle theorem / Apollonian gaskets in condensed matter.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: §4 of the paper proves $Q=2/3 \iff 4C = P$, i.e. the F-identity reduces to the Soddy/Descartes companion. This subfolder collects the CM-side precedents that already treat Apollonian/Descartes structures as physical (rather than purely mathematical) objects.

## Tier 1 (★ — primary CM-side citation)

- [Satija 2016 (EPJ ST)](satija_2016/one_pager.md) — *A tale of two fractals: The Hofstadter butterfly and the integral Apollonian gaskets,* EPJ ST 225 (2016) 2533 [arXiv:1606.09119]. Maps integer Apollonian gaskets (bends = √m-like integer curvatures) to the Hofstadter butterfly with a hidden D₃ symmetry — the closest published precedent for the κ=√m framework.

## Tier 1 (★ — book-length CM treatment)

- [Satija 2016 book](satija_2016_book/one_pager.md) — *Butterfly in the Quantum World* (IOP/Morgan & Claypool, 2016), ISBN 978-1-6817-4053-9. Single-citation gateway to Apollonian/Farey/Pythagorean trees in the IQHE.

## Tier 1 (★ — foam/granular CM application)

- [Andrade et al. 2005](andrade_herrmann_2005/one_pager.md) — *Apollonian networks,* PRL 94 (2005) 018702. Models dense granular packings, foam, and disordered materials as Apollonian packings; the foam-physics leg of the bridge.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Mackenzie 2010](mackenzie_2010/one_pager.md) — *A tisket, a tasket, an Apollonian gasket,* American Scientist 98 (2010) 10. Gateway article; explicit 2D foam / N(r) ∝ r^δ discussion.

## Secondary (†)

- [Yang & Liu 2017](yang_liu_2017/one_pager.md) — *Recursive sequences in the Ford sphere packing,* Chaos, Solitons & Fractals 104 (2017) 35. Useful for explicitly citing Satija's Apollonian↔Hofstadter mapping.
