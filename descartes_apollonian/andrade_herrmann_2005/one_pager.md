# Andrade, Herrmann, Andrade & da Silva 2005 — Apollonian networks

## Reference
J. S. Andrade Jr., H. J. Herrmann, R. F. S. Andrade, L. R. da Silva, *Apollonian networks: simultaneously scale-free, small world, Euclidean, space filling, and with matching graphs*, **Phys. Rev. Lett. 94 (2005) 018702**.

## Source
§1 (Descartes/Apollonian in CM), tier ★ — the CM-physics application of Apollonian packings to dense granular packings and foam.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Constructs networks based on the deterministic Apollonian gasket: each circle becomes a node, tangencies become edges. The resulting graph is simultaneously (i) scale-free with degree distribution $P(k) \sim k^{-\gamma}$ ($\gamma = 1 + \ln 3/\ln 2 \approx 2.585$), (ii) small-world with diameter $\sim \log\log N$, (iii) space-filling, and (iv) Euclidean (embeddable in 2D). Suggested as a model for foam topology, dense granular packings, and porous-media flow networks. Establishes Apollonian gaskets as legitimate CM-physics objects (not just mathematical curiosities).

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Concrete CM application of Apollonian packings beyond the Hofstadter butterfly link. Useful when emphasizing that the κ=√m circle construction is in a CM lineage that includes foam physics and granular matter — fields with established analytic-anchor traditions (Hashin–Shtrikman bounds, Matheron–Dykhne, etc.).
