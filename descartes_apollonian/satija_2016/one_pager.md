## Beyond-abstract summary
Maps the *integer* Apollonian gasket (the recursive packing in which all bends are integers, governed by the Descartes circle theorem) onto the Hofstadter butterfly — the spectrum of Bloch electrons in a 2D periodic potential with a perpendicular magnetic field. The mapping identifies the butterfly's "horizontal and vertical line ratios" with the bends of an integer Apollonian gasket, and reveals a hidden $D_3$ (i.e. $C_3 \rtimes Z_2$) symmetry "embedded in the kaleidoscopic images that describe the asymptotic scaling properties of the butterfly." A "symmetric-dual" gasket $(-\kappa_0^s, \kappa_1^s, \kappa_2^s, \kappa_3^s)$ is constructed from the butterfly data, with deviation $d = \frac{3q_L^2 - q_R^2}{2}$ from $D_3$ symmetry that vanishes as $q_R/q_L \to \sqrt{3}$. The Apollonian sequences $(4,15,56,209,780,2911,\ldots)$ represent the Chern-number hierarchy in the magnetic-flux interval $[1/3, 2/5]$.
