# Yang & Liu 2017 — Recursive sequences in the Ford sphere packing

## Reference

## Beyond-abstract summary
A mathematical-physics paper deriving recursive sequences in the Ford-sphere packing (a 3D generalization of the Apollonian gasket with circles replaced by spheres and the Farey tree replaced by the Stern–Brocot tree). Explicitly cites Satija's mapping between integer Apollonian packings and the Hofstadter problem as motivation; useful as a pointer paper for the Apollonian↔Hofstadter correspondence in the CM literature.
