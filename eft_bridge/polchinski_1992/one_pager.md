# Polchinski 1992 — Effective field theory and the Fermi surface

## Reference
J. Polchinski, *Effective field theory and the Fermi surface*, TASI 1992 lectures, **arXiv:hep-th/9210046** (October 1992); published in *Recent Directions in Particle Theory* (World Scientific, 1993).

## Source
§10 (EFT bridge), tier ✦ — companion to Shankar 1994; textbook statement of HEP-style EFT power counting applied to CM.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Pedagogical TASI lectures by Polchinski applying HEP effective-field-theory power counting to the Fermi-surface problem. Derives the BCS instability as the unique RG-relevant deformation of the Fermi-liquid fixed point — using exactly the HEP-style scaling arguments. The work is conceptually parallel to (and contemporaneous with) Shankar 1994 but emphasizes the HEP-EFT viewpoint and the analogy between "marginal" operators in HEP and the Landau-parameter sector in CM. Standard reference for "HEP EFT power counting works in CM."

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Companion citation to Shankar 1994 for the HEP-EFT-in-CM tradition. Useful when emphasizing that the cross-domain transport in the F-identity construction (CM mathematics applied to a HEP problem) is symmetric: HEP methods also import into CM productively.
