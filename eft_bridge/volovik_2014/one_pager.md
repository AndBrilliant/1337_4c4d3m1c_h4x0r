# Volovik 2014 — Higgs bosons in particle physics and in condensed matter

## Reference
G. E. Volovik, *Higgs bosons in particle physics and in condensed matter*, **J. Low Temp. Phys. 175 (2014) 486–497**; **arXiv:1305.7219**.

## Source
§10 (EFT bridge CM ↔ HEP), tier ★ — direct cross-domain EFT/Nambu-sum-rule application producing a mass relation.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Applies cross-domain EFT/Nambu-sum-rule machinery to predict Higgs partners. The Nambu sum rule (originating in superconductor pair-mode analysis) states $\sum_i M_i^2 = 4M_f^2$ for the fermion-pair modes; in the doubly-degenerate top-quark-condensation case (motivated by analogy with ³He-B Leggett modes), this becomes $2M_1^2 + 2M_2^2 = 4M_t^2$. Identifying $M_1 = 125$ GeV (the observed Higgs), the formula predicts $M_2 = 210$ GeV. The ³He-B analogy *motivates* the sum rule, but the 210 GeV value itself is from the top-condensation model, not from ³He directly. The paper is the cleanest single example of "HEP-style EFT producing a mass relation via a CM analogy."
