\section*{Demo paragraph}

Koide's empirical mass relation~\cite{koide1982} pins the charged-lepton
masses to a fixed quadratic invariant.  An entirely unrelated paper exists
that we accidentally cite here for the test~\cite{nonexistent_key}, and
the bibliography below also contains a bibitem we never cite (the orphan
case), plus a bibitem whose DOI points to a different paper than its title
claims~\cite{wrong_doi}.

\begin{thebibliography}{9}

\bibitem{koide1982}
Y.~Koide, \emph{Fermion-Boson Two-Body Model of Quarks and Leptons and
Cabibbo Mixing}, Lett.\ Nuovo Cim.\ \textbf{34} (1982) 201--205,
\textsc{doi}:10.1007/BF02817096.

\bibitem{orphan_pass}
J.~M.~Maldacena, \emph{The Large-N limit of superconformal field theories
and supergravity}, Adv.\ Theor.\ Math.\ Phys.\ \textbf{2} (1998) 231,
arXiv:hep-th/9711200.

\bibitem{wrong_doi}
S.~Hawking, \emph{Particle Creation by Black Holes}, Commun.\ Math.\ Phys.\
\textbf{43} (1975) 199--220, \textsc{doi}:10.1038/nature12373.

\end{thebibliography}
