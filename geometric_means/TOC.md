# geometric_means/ — Table of Contents

> §5 of the source report: *Geometric means in effective-medium theory.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: §6 uses two geometric-mean cascade steps — $m_s^2 = \mu_\star \cdot m_d$ and $m_u^2 = m_d \cdot 2m_e$ — to extend the F-identity companion to $m_d$ and $m_u$. This subfolder collects the CM precedents in which a *geometric mean* is the exact physical answer for a mixed-medium parameter.

## Tier 1 (★ — Matheron–Dykhne theorem, primary citation)

- [Dykhne 1971](dykhne_1971/one_pager.md) — *Conductivity of a 2D two-phase system,* Sov. Phys. JETP 32 (1971) 63 [Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fiz. 59 (1970) 110]. σ_eff = √(σ₁σ₂) for isotropic 2D two-phase composites at equal volume fraction.

## Tier 1 (★ — earlier porous-media derivation)

- [Matheron 1967](matheron_1967/one_pager.md) — *Éléments pour une Théorie des Milieux Poreux* (Masson, Paris, 1967). The "Matheron conjecture" — same theorem, French-language monograph.

## Tier 1 (★ — Hashin–Shtrikman bounds)

- [Hashin & Shtrikman 1963](hashin_shtrikman_1963/one_pager.md) — *Elastic behaviour of multiphase materials,* J. Mech. Phys. Solids 11 (1963) 127. Tightest variational bounds expressible in constituent properties.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Hashin & Shtrikman 1962](hashin_shtrikman_1962/one_pager.md) — *Effective magnetic permeability of multiphase materials,* J. Appl. Phys. 33 (1962) 3125. Companion conductivity/permeability bounds.
- [Milton 2002](milton_2002/one_pager.md) — *The Theory of Composites* (Cambridge, 2002). Standard modern monograph; 2D geometric-mean exact relations.
