# Hashin & Shtrikman 1962 — Effective magnetic permeability / conductivity bounds

## Reference
Z. Hashin, S. Shtrikman, *A variational approach to the theory of the effective magnetic permeability of multiphase materials*, **J. Appl. Phys. 33 (1962) 3125–3131**. No arXiv (1962).

## Source
§5 (Geometric means in EMT), tier ✦ — companion paper to Hashin–Shtrikman 1963 for the scalar (conductivity / permeability) case.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Companion to Hashin–Shtrikman (1963 JMPS) but for *scalar* transport coefficients (electrical conductivity, magnetic permeability, dielectric constant — all governed by the same Laplace-type equation). Establishes the tight variational bounds $\sigma_{\rm HS}^{\pm}$ on the effective conductivity of an isotropic two-phase composite, expressed in constituent conductivities and volume fractions alone. The bounds are achieved by specific microgeometries (coated-sphere or coated-cylinder assemblages). For equal volume fractions in 2D, the HS bounds collapse to a single value — the Matheron–Dykhne geometric mean $\sqrt{\sigma_1\sigma_2}$.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Supporting citation alongside HS-1963 (elastic) and Dykhne 1971 (electrical, equal-volume-fraction 2D). Establishes the breadth of "constituent-property-only" effective-medium results.
