# Matheron 1967 — Éléments pour une Théorie des Milieux Poreux

## Reference
G. Matheron, **Éléments pour une Théorie des Milieux Poreux** (Masson, Paris, 1967), 166 pp. French-language monograph; no English translation; no arXiv.

## Source
§5 (Geometric means in effective-medium theory), tier ★ — the earlier (1967) derivation of the 2D two-phase geometric-mean theorem ("Matheron conjecture"); jointly cited with Dykhne 1971 as the "Matheron–Dykhne theorem."

## Beyond-abstract summary
A monograph on porous-media flow and effective conductivity, written in the context of 1960s geostatistics. The relevant theoretical result: for a 2D random isotropic two-phase medium with equal volume fractions, the effective conductivity equals the geometric mean $\sigma_{\rm eff} = \sqrt{\sigma_1\sigma_2}$. Matheron derived this from a self-similarity / duality argument that prefigured Dykhne's 1971 reformulation. The work also pioneered the application of probability theory and stochastic geometry to porous media — establishing the now-standard "geostatistics" subfield.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Joint citation with Dykhne 1971 for the geometric-mean exact relation. Useful when emphasizing that the 2D two-phase $\sqrt{\sigma_1\sigma_2}$ theorem has *two* independent derivations (Matheron probabilistic / porous-media, Dykhne duality / electromagnetic) — establishing it as a robust CM precedent for "geometric mean = exact answer."
