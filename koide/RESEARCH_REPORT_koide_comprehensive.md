# Comprehensive Literature Review of the Koide Lepton Mass Relation (1982–2026)

> **Source:** Claude web research mode output, retrieved 2026-05-08. Filed under `koide/` because the report is a 1982-onward survey of Koide-relation phenomenology, derivations, and extensions.

## TL;DR
- The Koide relation Q = (m_e + m_μ + m_τ)/(√m_e + √m_μ + √m_τ)² = 2/3 has held to ≈10⁻⁵ for over 40 years; with PDG-aligned charged-lepton pole masses Q ≈ 0.666661 ± 0.000007, and the Belle II 2023 measurement (m_τ = 1777.09 ± 0.08 ± 0.11 MeV, arXiv:2305.19116) keeps the world average compatible with 2/3 within ~1σ.
- Despite three lineages of derivation attempts — Koide's S₃/U(3)/democratic-matrix models, Sumino's family-gauge cancellation of QED running, and various discrete-flavor / yukawaon / geometric reformulations (Foot, Kocik, Brannen, Sheppeard) — no derivation has achieved both (i) the 10⁻⁵ pole-mass precision and (ii) a falsifiable extension that survives the quark sector intact, which is the open gap a C₃×Z₂ geometric mechanism would need to close.
- Extensions to quarks succeed only with sign choices and triplet selections (Rodejohann–Zhang heavy (c,b,t); Rivero's strange–charm–bottom; Kartavtsev's six-quark sum) and to neutrinos only with one negative √m (Brannen 2006), strongly suggesting Koide is a *charged-lepton-specific* coincidence whose origin must respect the discrete Z₃ family structure.

## Key Findings

**Origin and exact precision.** The relation was first written down in Y. Koide, *Lett. Nuovo Cim.* **34**, 201 (1982) ("Fermion-boson two body model of quarks and leptons and Cabibbo mixing") and further developed in Y. Koide, *Phys. Rev. D* **28**, 252 (1983) (erratum *PRD* **29**, 1544 (1984)) and *Phys. Lett. B* **120**, 161 (1983). The original 1982 paper *predicted* m_τ ≈ 1.777 GeV when the world-average tau mass was 1.7842 GeV — a >2σ deficit at the time; the prediction was vindicated by J. Z. Bai et al. (BES Collaboration), *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **69**, 3021 (1992), measuring m_τ = 1776.9 +0.4/−0.5 ± 0.2 MeV at BEPC (updated with more decay channels in *Phys. Rev. D* **53**, 20 (1996)). The PDG 2024 / Belle II 2023 inputs now place Q within 10⁻⁵ of 2/3.

**Three derivation strategies have been attempted, none fully successful.** (a) Discrete-flavor / democratic mass matrices (Koide 1989, 1995, 1999; Xing 2010; Kleppe 2025); (b) family-gauge radiative cancellation (Sumino 2009; Koide–Yamashita 2012; Koide 2016, 2017); (c) algebraic / geometric reformulations (Foot 1994; Brannen 2006; Kocik 2012; Sheppeard via viXra). The C₃×Z₂ geometric mechanism this report supports falls into a fourth, less-explored lineage that takes the Z₃ Fourier structure (already noted by Brannen and Sheppeard) seriously as the *primary* explanation rather than a parametrization.

### 2. Parametrizations and extensions

**Carl Brannen (2006).** The single most influential post-Koide reformulation. In the unpublished preprints "Koide Mass Formula for Neutrinos" (brannenworks.com/MASSES.pdf, presented at APS NW 2006, Bull. APS) and "The Lepton Masses" (brannenworks.com/MASSES2.pdf, 2006), Brannen wrote the charged-lepton square-roots as eigenvalues of a real-eigenvalued circulant matrix:
- √m_n = μ[1 + √2 η cos(δ + 2πn/3)], n = 0,1,2
- Fit: η² = 0.500003(23) (i.e., η² = 1/2 ↔ Q = 2/3 exactly) and **δ ≈ 0.22222204717(48), strikingly close to 2/9 rad**.
- For neutrinos: same form with δ → δ + π/12, and a sign flip on the lightest √m_ν, giving μ_ν/μ_e = 1/3¹².

Brannen's δ ≈ 2/9 has driven much subsequent work (Żenczykowski 2012, 2013, 2018; recent G₂-Casimir conjectures). His preprints have circulated on physicsforums and viXra rather than arXiv; consequently they appear in INSPIRE only as "as cited in" through Rivero–Gsponer.

**Alejandro Rivero.**
- *hep-ph/0505220* (with A. Gsponer, 2005), "The strange formula of Dr. Koide" — historical and bibliographical review; the canonical English-language entry point. Observation paper; introduces the "gim" notation J²=m to remove square roots, points out Koide-like fits for down-quarks (b,s,d) with Barut masses.
- *arXiv:1111.7232* (2011), "A new Koide tuple: strange-charm-bottom" — observes that with √m_s taken with negative sign, (s,c,b) form a Koide triplet quasi-orthogonal to (e,μ,τ). Proposes "Koide waterfall": chaining s-c-b → c-b-t → ... gives m_t ≈ 173.26 GeV.
- Rivero (2014–15) talks/notes (researchgate publication 275410011, "Koide formula: beyond charged leptons", slideshare/koide2014talk, in2p3 video) extending the waterfall and discussing dual / inverse-mass and Markov-tree constructions where Q(1/m_d, 1/m_s, 1/m_b) ≈ 2/3.
- **A claimed "Koide with AI" 2026 preprint by Rivero on ai.viXra.org could not be located via web search and remains unconfirmed.** His confirmed Koide-related arXiv contributions stop at 1111.7232 (2011).

### 3. Heavy-quark Koide tuples

- **Rodejohann & Zhang**, "Extension of an empirical charged lepton mass relation to the neutrino sector," arXiv:1101.5525 → *Phys. Lett. B* **698**, 152 (2011). v1 contained an explicit observation that Q ≈ 2/3 holds for both light (u,d,s) and heavy (c,b,t) quark triplets; the heavy-triplet remark was **removed in the published version**, so the first peer-reviewed mention belongs to Cao.
- **F. G. Cao**, "Neutrino masses from lepton and quark mass relations and neutrino oscillations," arXiv:1205.4068 → *Phys. Rev. D* **85**, 113003 (2012) — first published quotation of Q_heavy = (m_c+m_b+m_t)/(√m_c+√m_b+√m_t)² ≈ 0.669, and inverts Koide-like cross-sector relations to predict m_1 ≈ 2×10⁻⁴ eV (with large uncertainty), m_2 = (8.7±0.1)×10⁻³ eV, m_3 = (4.9±0.1)×10⁻² eV with normal hierarchy strongly preferred.
- **A. Kartavtsev**, "A remark on the Koide relation for quarks," arXiv:1111.0480 (Nov 2011) — proposes the **six-quark democratic sum** K_q = (Σ_q m_q)/(Σ_q √m_q)² ≈ 2/3, finding the relation insensitive to RG running.
- **Gao & Li**, "Explorations of two empirical formulae for fermion masses," arXiv:1512.06349 → *Eur. Phys. J. C* **76**, 140 (2016) — finds K_q is RG-stable from GeV up to 10¹² GeV, but always ~5% above 2/3.
- **Rivero 1111.7232** (above) provides the "middle" tuple (-√m_s, √m_c, √m_b) with Q ≈ 0.675.

### 4. Geometric approaches

- **R. Foot**, "A note on Koide's lepton mass relation," arXiv:hep-ph/9402242 (1994). Observation: Koide is equivalent to ∠((√m_e,√m_μ,√m_τ),(1,1,1)) = π/4. Has not pursued a discrete-group derivation; his mirror-symmetry program is on a separate track.
- **Brannen** (2006) — extends Foot's geometry to an eigenvalue equation; phase δ = 2/9 noted (above).
- **J. Kocik**, "The Koide Lepton Mass Formula and Geometry of Circles," arXiv:1201.2067 (2012). Reads Koide as a generalized Descartes–Apollonius circle relation: with curvatures b_i = √m_i, a Descartes-like quadratic with coefficient p=2/3 holds. Mathematically suggestive but not a dynamical derivation.
- **Marni Sheppeard** (viXra: 0711.0001, 1010.0029, and ~30 subsequent notes 2007–2017) — circulant matrices, category-theoretic / twistor reformulations, and the explicit identification that Brannen's parametrization is a **discrete Fourier transform on Z_3** of the √m vector. Per New Zealand Police statements (March 2021), Sheppeard was last heard from on Twitter on 19 November 2020 when she said she was "off to the mountains"; human remains believed to be hers were found in the Rolleston River area, Otira, West Coast in March 2021. Her work largely on viXra and not in INSPIRE; influenced Brannen's joint program.

### 5. Theoretical derivations (mechanisms)

**Koide's S₃ democratic models** (1989 → 2012) — see table above. Achieves Koide at tree level under specific democratic-matrix textures, but radiative corrections are not protected.

**Sumino mechanism — the most-cited theoretical-derivation thread.**
- Y. Sumino, "Family gauge symmetry and Koide's mass formula," arXiv:0812.2090 → *Phys. Lett. B* **671**, 477 (2009). Verbatim from the abstract: the cancellation is "discussed in an effective theory with U(3) family gauge symmetry and a scenario in which this symmetry is unified with SU(2)_L symmetry at 10²–10³ TeV scale."
- Y. Sumino, "Family gauge symmetry as an origin of Koide's mass formula and charged lepton spectrum," arXiv:0812.2103 → *JHEP* 0905:075 (2009).
- Y. Sumino, "Family gauge symmetry as an origin..." (review/talk), arXiv:0903.3640 (2009).
The mechanism: gauge a U(3) family symmetry; arrange (M_ij)² ∝ (m_i + m_j) for family gauge bosons; the FGB radiative correction log(M_ii/μ)² cancels the QED log(m_i/μ)² in the pole-mass running, so Koide can be valid simultaneously at high scale **and** for pole masses.

**Koide's response to Sumino** (1004.0580, 1009.4254, 1106.0971, 1110.5413, 1203.2028, 1209.1694, 1309.4172, 1405.6778, 1507.04987, 1608.04514, 1701.01921, 1809.00425) — yukawaon variants, SU(5)-compatible Yukawaon (with U(3)×O(3)), inverted-hierarchy SUSY-compatible variant (Koide–Yamashita 2012), anomaly-free version (Koide 2016).

**RG running studies.**
- Z. Xing & H. Zhang, "On the Koide-like relations for the running masses of charged leptons, neutrinos and quarks," hep-ph/0602134 → *Phys. Lett. B* **635**, 107 (2006). Q_l(M_Z) deviates by 0.2% from Q_l^pole; Q_U(M_Z) ≈ 0.89, Q_D(M_Z) ≈ 0.74; nearly RG-stable to 10¹⁴ GeV, but never near 2/3.
- N. Li & B.-Q. Ma, hep-ph/0601031 (2006) → *Phys. Rev. D* **73**, 013009; estimate of neutrino masses, hep-ph/0505028 (2005) → *Phys. Lett. B* **609**, 309. Quark-lepton complementarity k_l + k_d ≈ k_ν + k_u ≈ 2.

**Discrete-flavor / scalar-potential derivations beyond Koide–Sumino.**
- **Gérard, Goffinet, Herquet**, "A new look at an old mass relation," hep-ph/0510289 → *Phys. Lett. B* **633**, 563 (2006). Extension to all four sectors; predicts a ~3×10⁻² eV Dirac mass for the lightest neutrino and rules out maximal atmospheric mixing.
- **F. Goffinet** PhD thesis, "A bottom-up approach to fermion masses" (UCLouvain 2008) — pole-mass treatment, cubic reformulation avoiding √m.
- **Haba et al.** hep-ph/0005064 — democratic-matrix radiative stability for leptons.
- **Z. Xing**, broken-S₃ flavor symmetry, arXiv:1004.4234 (2010) — ξI + ζD pattern.
- **P. Żenczykowski**, *Phys. Rev. D* **86**, 117303 (2012) (arXiv:1210.4125); *PRD* **87**, 077302 (2013) (arXiv:1301.4143); arXiv:1809 (Doubly-special parametrization, 2018) — argues low-energy phase parameters δ_L = 3δ_D/2 = 3δ_U = 2/9, with k_L = 1.
- **Liang & Sun**, "A modified version of the Koide formula from flavor nonets in a scalar potential model and in a Yukawaon model," arXiv:2007.05878 (2020–2021) — derivation from SU(3) flavor-nonet VEV with two effective parameters that fit charged leptons and both quark sectors.

**Foot–Lew, Adler, Kiselev, Krolikowski.** Adler's PRD 59, 015012 (1999) (hep-ph/9806518) on spontaneously broken discrete chiral symmetries; Kiselev hep-ph/9806523; Krolikowski hep-ph/0508039 — all are derivation attempts cited in Rivero–Gsponer; none are widely viewed as having succeeded. **No string-theory or extra-dimensional derivation of the Koide relation has appeared in the peer-reviewed literature.** Recent claims at zenodo.org/19271373 ("Koide as Cogito" / SO(6) compactification) and substack posts ("modular invariance forces Q=2/3") are non-peer-reviewed essays and should not be cited as supporting evidence.

### 6. Empirical status (2024–2026)

- **PDG values used:** m_e = 0.51099895000(15) MeV, m_μ = 105.6583755(23) MeV, m_τ = 1776.86 ± 0.12 MeV (PDG 2024 average dominated by pre-2023 data).
- **Belle II measurement (arXiv:2305.19116, 2023):** m_τ = 1777.09 ± 0.08(stat) ± 0.11(syst) MeV — most precise single measurement; pulls the world average upward.
- **Resulting Q:** Q = 0.666661 ± 0.000007, i.e. 2/3 × (0.99999 ± 0.00001), compatible with 2/3 at ~1σ. The Belle II central value alone gives Q ≈ 2/3 + ~1×10⁻⁵.
- The pole-mass relation has *strengthened* over 40 years: 1982 prediction was 2σ off; 2024 it is consistent with 2/3 to a few parts in 10⁶ when using the inverse-error-weighted average of post-2014 measurements.

### 7. "Accidental" vs. meaningful

The mathematical bound is 1/3 < Q < 1, so Q = 2/3 is exactly in the middle of the allowed range — a fact emphasized as suspicious-but-suggestive by Foot and as evidence-of-numerology by skeptics (e.g., John Baez, "The Koide Formula," Azimuth blog, 4 April 2021, https://johncarlosbaez.wordpress.com/2021/04/04/the-koide-formula/, and Lubos Motl's "Could the Koide formula be real?" The Reference Frame, 16 January 2012). No proper null-model significance analysis has been published. Q being a midpoint and the simultaneous "accident" δ ≈ 2/9 (Brannen) are typically taken together as the empirical input to be explained.

### 8. Extensions beyond charged leptons

- **Neutrinos:** Brannen 2006 (with √m_ν1 < 0); Li–Ma 2005 (k_ν ~ 0.33–0.57 without sign flip); Cao 2012 PRD prediction; Huang et al. 1601.00754 (seesaw + Koide). Gérard–Goffinet–Herquet predicts m_1 ≈ 3×10⁻² eV. Recent (2025) PMNS derivation in preprints.org/202504.1361.
- **Quark triples:** Rodejohann–Zhang light/heavy; Rivero (s,c,b) and waterfall to top; Kartavtsev 6-quark.
- **Universal Koide:** Kartavtsev's six-quark sum; Xing–Zhang RG analysis; Liang–Sun 2020 modified Koide fits all three charged sectors with two parameters.
- **Cross-sector / quark-lepton:** Li–Ma k_l + k_d ≈ k_ν + k_u ≈ 2; Cao 2012; Kleppe arXiv:2504.02104 (April 2025) "Quark mass matrices inspired by a numerical relation" — constructs democratic-texture quark mass matrices using the Koide ansatz on √m eigenvalues.

## Recommendations

For a paper proposing a **C₃ × Z₂ geometric mechanism** for Koide:

1. **Cite as the empirical anchor**: Koide 1982 (Lett. Nuovo Cim. 34, 201), Koide 1983 (PRD 28, 252), Foot 1994 (hep-ph/9402242) for the 45° interpretation, Brannen 2006 for the Z₃-Fourier δ = 2/9 phase, the BES 1992 vindication (Bai et al., PRL 69, 3021), and the **PDG 2024 + Belle II 2305.19116 numerical value Q = 0.666661 ± 0.000007**.

2. **Position against three competitor mechanisms**, explicitly:
   - **Sumino's family-gauge cancellation** (0812.2090, 0812.2103) is the gold-standard mechanism in the literature; explain why a geometric C₃ mechanism is preferable (no 10²–10³ TeV family gauge bosons required, no anomaly issues à la Koide–Yamashita 2012, 2016).
   - **Koide's yukawaon / democratic S₃ models** (1989–2018, esp. hep-ph/9905416, 1004.0580, 1106.0971) are the canonical Koide-side mechanism but require fine-tuned superpotentials.
   - **Discrete-flavor scalar-potential models** (Gérard-Goffinet-Herquet 2006, Liang-Sun 2020) — closest to a "pure symmetry" derivation. A C₃×Z₂ paper should explicitly contrast its symmetry content with these.

3. **Acknowledge and use the Z₃ Fourier structure** noted by Sheppeard and Brannen, and the Apollonian-circle reading by Kocik (1201.2067). These are the closest precedents to a geometric argument.

4. **Address the quark sector explicitly.** A geometric mechanism that explains charged leptons but cannot say *why* up-down quarks fail Koide while (c,b,t) succeed at ~10⁻² will be vulnerable. Cite Rodejohann–Zhang 1101.5525, Cao 2012 PRD, Kartavtsev 1111.0480, Rivero 1111.7232.

5. **Address running.** Cite Xing–Zhang hep-ph/0602134 and N. Li–Ma hep-ph/0601031: a charged-lepton mechanism must explain why the relation is exact for **pole** masses (the 0.2% deviation at M_Z is the key signature distinguishing pole-Koide from running-Koide). Either invoke a Sumino-style cancellation or argue your mechanism naturally lives at the IR pole-mass scale.

6. **Benchmarks that would change the recommendations:**
   - If a future tau-mass measurement pushes Q outside 2/3 ± 5×10⁻⁵, the relation is dead and so is any geometric mechanism explaining it. **Threshold: |Q − 2/3| > 5×10⁻⁵ at 5σ.**
   - If LHC / Belle II find a TeV-scale family gauge boson with the Koide–Yamashita inverted-hierarchy spectrum, Sumino-class mechanisms gain a definitive edge over a pure-geometry mechanism.
   - If the Brannen δ deviates from 2/9 by more than 10⁻³ in updated PDG data, Z₃-Fourier-based mechanisms (including C₃×Z₂) lose their primary empirical hook.
