# Literature Review: The Koide Lepton Mass Relation, 1982–2026

> **Source:** Claude web research mode output, retrieved 2026-05-08. Filed under `koide/` because the report is a comprehensive Koide-relation literature review with mechanism analysis.

## TL;DR
- **Forty-four years on, no microscopic derivation of Q ≡ (mₑ + m_μ + m_τ)/(√mₑ + √m_μ + √m_τ)² = 2/3 has been universally accepted; the strongest mechanistic claim remains Yukinari Sumino's 2008–2009 U(3) family gauge model, which protects the relation by cancelling the QED radiative correction between pole and running masses in "an effective theory with U(3) family gauge symmetry and a scenario in which this symmetry is unified with SU(2)_L symmetry at 10²–10³ TeV scale" (Sumino, *Phys. Lett. B* 671 (2009) 477; arXiv:0812.2090). The geometric/phase reading (Foot 1994; Brannen 2006; Kocik 2012) and the discrete-flavor reading (Koide's S₃ / yukawaon program; Ma 2007) are alternative paradigms.**
- **Empirically, with the PDG 2024 charged-lepton masses (mₑ = 0.51099895000(15) MeV, m_μ = 105.6583755(23) MeV, m_τ = 1776.93(09) MeV — the m_τ uncertainty pulled tight by the Belle II 2023 pseudomass measurement 1777.09 ± 0.08 ± 0.11 MeV from 190 fb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ → τ⁺τ⁻), Q = 0.66666446(508), differing from 2/3 by ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻⁶. The relation is exact at the < 10⁻⁵ level for pole masses and has tracked every refinement of m_τ from the 1992 BES revision onward.**
- **Extensions to other triples (Brannen's neutrinos with √m₁ → −√m₁; Rodejohann–Zhang's c-b-t; Rivero's s-c-b with sign flip; Kartavtsev's 6-fermion sum) are at best 0.1–5% accurate, none with the precision of the charged-lepton case — making the charged-lepton triple genuinely special and a C₃ × Z₂ symmetry hypothesis well-targeted at that triple.**

## Key Findings

1. **Original Koide papers (1982–1983).** The relation was first written in Y. Koide, "Fermion–boson two-body model of quarks and leptons and Cabibbo mixing," *Lett. Nuovo Cim.* **34** (1982) 201 (DOI 10.1007/BF02817096), as Eq. (17) inside a fermion–boson preon model that also tried to predict CKM angles and quark masses (most of those side-predictions are now ruled out). It was restated more cleanly in Y. Koide, "A fermion–boson composite model of quarks and leptons," *Phys. Lett. B* **120** (1983) 161 (DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(83)90644-5) and in Y. Koide, "New view of quark and lepton mass hierarchy," *Phys. Rev. D* **28** (1983) 252 (with erratum *Phys. Rev. D* **29** (1984) 1544). Koide's tau prediction was 1776.97 MeV at a time when the world-average tau mass was about 1784 MeV (the Rivero–Gsponer review cites the contemporary value as "1.7842 GeV/c², more than two standard deviations away"). The experimental value migrated to Koide's prediction over 1992–1996 (BES, ARGUS, CLEO).

2. **Koide's own multi-decade program.** Koide spent four decades attempting to derive the relation: (i) U(3)-family Higgs scalar potential, *Mod. Phys. Lett. A* **5** (1990) 2319; (ii) nonet Higgs / U(3) family symmetry, hep-ph/9501408 (1995); (iii) seesaw quark-mass extensions with H. Fusaoka, *Z. Phys. C* **71** (1996) 459 (hep-ph/9505201) and "A democratic seesaw quark mass matrix related to the charged lepton masses," hep-ph/9602303; (iv) "Universal seesaw mass matrix model with an S₃ symmetry," *Phys. Rev. D* **60** (1999) 077301 (hep-ph/9905416); (v) "Quark and lepton mass matrices with a cyclic permutation invariant form," hep-ph/0005137 (2000); (vi) the major review/manifesto "Challenge to the Mystery of the Charged Lepton Mass Formula," hep-ph/0506247 (2005), which proposed Z₄ × S₃ symmetry on flavor-triplet scalars; (vii) "Tribimaximal Neutrino Mixing and a Relation Between Neutrino- and Charged Lepton-Mass Spectra" (hep-ph/0605074, 2006) and arXiv:0706.2534 (2007), the first papers to cite Brannen's δ = 2/9 result; (viii) "S₃ symmetry and neutrino masses and mixings," *Eur. Phys. J. C* **50** (2007) 809; (ix) "A₄ symmetry and lepton masses and mixing," hep-ph/0701018 (2007); (x) the *yukawaon* program (2008–2013) where the Yukawa coupling becomes the VEV of a 3×3 scalar — *Phys. Rev. D* **78** (2008) 093006 (arXiv:0809.2449), *Phys. Rev. D* **79** (2009) 033009 (arXiv:0811.3470), arXiv:0904.1644, 1001.2075, 1011.1064, *Phys. Lett. B* **712** (2012) 396 (arXiv:1202.5815, with Nishiura), arXiv:1308.2129; (xi) "New U(3) Family Gauge Symmetry and Muonium into Antimuonium Conversion," arXiv:1009.4254, endorsing Sumino's mechanism. None of these models is universally accepted as the explanation.

3. **The geometric reading (Foot 1994).** R. Foot, "A note on Koide's lepton mass relation," arXiv:hep-ph/9402242 (1994) — an arXiv-only McGill preprint, never journal-published — showed that Q = 2/3 is equivalent to the angle between (√mₑ, √m_μ, √m_τ) and (1,1,1) being exactly 45° / cos²θ = 1/2. This 45° mnemonic is now used by virtually every subsequent paper. Foot was cautious: "It could be that the mass formula eq.(1) is of no fundamental significance since it is possible that its empirical success is just a numerical coincidence." Observation, not mechanism.

4. **The cosine/circulant parametrization (Brannen 2006).** Carl Brannen — never an arXiv author by virtue of moderator removal; his work appeared on his personal site brannenworks.com (MASSES.pdf, April 2006; MASSES2.pdf, 2 May 2006), in Bulletin of the APS abstracts (Brannen 2006, BAPS), and on viXra (e.g. viXra:0702.0052) — wrote √mₖ = √μ (1 + √2 cos(δ + 2πk/3)) for k = 0,1,2, showed that Koide is automatic when the prefactor is √2, and found that the empirical phase for charged leptons is δ ≈ 0.222222 ≈ 2/9 to 5 decimal places. Brannen further conjectured the *neutrino* version with the sign of √m₁ flipped: m₁ + m₂ + m₃ = (2/3)(−√m₁ + √m₂ + √m₃)². Koide then took the conjecture seriously in hep-ph/0605074 (2006). Brannen's parametrization is the single most-used representation of Koide in the geometric/discrete-Fourier literature, and the joint coincidence of Q = 2/3 *and* δ = 2/9 is a stronger empirical constraint than Q = 2/3 alone. Mechanism: a circulant Hermitian mass matrix Γ(A,B,C); colored-preon interpretation. With M. D. Sheppeard he wrote "Colored Preons," arXiv:0901.1687.

5. **Z₃ Fourier / circulant matrices (Sheppeard).** Marni D. Sheppeard (a New Zealand mathematician, deceased 2018) developed circulant 3×3 mass-matrix and category-theory readings, almost entirely on viXra (e.g. viXra:0711.0001, viXra:1711.0336 "Lepton mass phases and the CKM matrix"). Brannen credits her with the observation that Koide's parametrization is precisely the discrete Fourier transform on Z₃, and that the CKM matrix decomposes as a 1-circulant + 2-circulant. She explored ribbon/category-theoretic interpretations and a 6D qubit ⊗ qutrit tensor structure. Almost all peer-reviewed citations of Sheppeard's Koide work go through Brannen's papers.

6. **Rivero's program.** A. Rivero & A. Gsponer, "The strange formula of Dr. Koide," arXiv:hep-ph/0505220 (May 2005; never journal-published), is the standard historical/bibliographic review of Koide; the Semantic Scholar record (corpus ID 16583689) shows it is well-cited in the field, though a verified live citation total was not obtained in this review and should be checked on INSPIRE. Rivero independently proposed (Physics Forums 2011 / arXiv:1111.7232 "A new Koide tuple: strange–charm–bottom," unpublished) the s-c-b triplet using a sign flip on √mₛ, complementing Rodejohann–Zhang's c-b-t observation, and described it as "quasi-orthogonal to the original lepton triplet." His "Koide waterfall" presentation argues for nested cascades of triplets (e-μ-τ, s-c-b, c-b-t) with shared phase structure. Rivero has continued posting (e.g. arXiv:2407.05397, "An interpretation of scalars in SO(32)," *Eur. Phys. J. C* **84** (2024) 1058, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-024-13368-3). **No peer-reviewed "Koide with AI" paper by Rivero on arXiv was located through May 2026**; an unrelated 2026 ai.viXra note (2602.0108v1) on a G₂ Casimir derivation of δ = 2/9 by another author exists and was discussed on Physics Forums (thread 1084336).

7. **Heavy-quark Koide.** W. Rodejohann & H. Zhang, "Extension of an empirical charged lepton mass relation to the neutrino sector," arXiv:1101.5525 v1 (Jan 2011), *Phys. Lett. B* **698** (2011) 152, noted in v1 that the Koide ratio for (c,b,t) is ≈ 0.66–0.68 ("for the c, b, t quarks") — but this c-b-t observation was *removed from the published version*. A. Kartavtsev, "A remark on the Koide relation for quarks," arXiv:1111.0480 (Nov 2011, never journal-published) proposed the universal 6-fermion extension K_l = (Σm_l)/(Σ√m_l)² = 2/3 over all 6 leptons, and similarly K_q for all 6 quarks. F. G. Cao, "Neutrino masses from lepton and quark mass relations and neutrino oscillations," arXiv:1205.4068, *Phys. Rev. D* **85** (2012) 113003 (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.85.113003), was the first journal-published heavy-quark Koide statement and used it (with neutrino oscillation data) to predict absolute neutrino masses m₁ = 0.21⁺¹·⁷⁰₋₀.₂₁ × 10⁻⁴ eV, m₂ = (8.7 ± 0.1) × 10⁻³ eV, m₃ = (4.9 ± 0.1) × 10⁻² eV under the normal hierarchy.

8. **Geometry of circles (Kocik 2012).** J. Kocik (Southern Illinois University), "The Koide Lepton Mass Formula and Geometry of Circles," arXiv:1201.2067 (physics.gen-ph; arXiv-only) showed that Koide's relation is *formally* a generalized Descartes circle theorem: in the standard Descartes formula (a+b+c+d)² = 2(a²+b²+c²+d²) the coefficient is 2; reading curvatures bᵢ = 1/√mᵢ and dropping the fourth circle gives Koide's relation with coefficient 2/3. Kocik characterizes this as three coplanar circles meeting pairwise at the same angle φ = arccos(2/3) ≈ 48.2°, equivalent to Q = 2/3, and gives an n-dimensional generalization for n+2 spheres satisfying Σbᵢ² = (p/((n+1)p−1))(Σbᵢ)², with κ = 2/3 ⇒ p = 2/(2n−1). His work also accommodates Brannen's neutrino sign flip via negative curvature (unbounded disks). Predecessor: S. Esposito & P. Santorelli, "A Geometric Picture for Fermion Masses," hep-ph/9603369, *Mod. Phys. Lett. A* **10** (1995) 3077.

9. **The Sumino mechanism — the most serious dynamical proposal.** Y. Sumino, "Family gauge symmetry and Koide's mass formula," arXiv:0812.2090, *Phys. Lett. B* **671** (2009) 477 (DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2008.12.060) — abstract explicitly: "We propose a mechanism for cancelling the QED correction to Koide's formula. This is discussed in an effective theory with U(3) family gauge symmetry and a scenario in which this symmetry is unified with SU(2)_L symmetry at 10²–10³ TeV scale" — and the longer Y. Sumino, "Family Gauge Symmetry as an Origin of Koide's Mass Formula and Charged Lepton Spectrum," arXiv:0812.2103, *JHEP* **0905** (2009) 075 (DOI 10.1088/1126-6708/2009/05/075), with summary 0903.3640. The motivation is the puzzle made sharp by Z.-Z. Xing & H. Zhang, "On the Koide-like Relations for the Running Masses of Charged Leptons, Neutrinos and Quarks," hep-ph/0602134, *Phys. Lett. B* **635** (2006) 107: the running Koide ratio at M_Z is K^run(M_Z) = (2/3) × (1.00189 ± 0.00002) — i.e. the running relation deviates from 2/3 by ≈ 0.2%, far outside the 10⁻⁵ pole-mass precision. Either Q = 2/3 holds for pole masses *and* a Sumino-like cancellation aligns this with high-scale physics, or Q = 2/3 holds at some special high scale and one must explain the apparent low-scale agreement as a coincidence. Note that N. Li & B.-Q. Ma, "Energy scale independence of Koide's relation for quark and lepton masses," hep-ph/0601031, *Phys. Rev. D* **73** (2006) 013009, reach a slightly different conclusion (energy-scale stability); the field has not fully converged on this question. SUSY versions of Sumino were developed (Koide 1009.4254 and follow-ups) because the Sumino mechanism cannot straightforwardly be applied to SUSY models due to the non-renormalization theorem.

10. **Discrete-flavor derivations.** E. Ma, "Lepton family symmetry and possible application to the Koide mass formula," hep-ph/0612022, *Phys. Lett. B* **649** (2007) 287, used a finite group generated by four Z₃ transformations to obtain mᵢ ∝ vᵢ², which is the structural prerequisite for Koide. Koide's hep-ph/0701018 ("A₄ symmetry and lepton masses and mixing") parallels Ma's A₄ approach. Other relevant flavor-symmetry derivations include V. V. Kiselev, "Model for three generations of fermions," hep-ph/9806523, and S. L. Adler, "Model for particle masses, flavor mixing, and CP violation, based on spontaneously broken discrete chiral symmetry as the origin of families," *Phys. Rev. D* **59** (1999) 015012 (hep-ph/9806518), where retrocirculant Higgs structures naturally produce Koide-like sum rules. P. Żenczykowski, "Remark on Koide's Z₃-symmetric parametrization of quark masses," arXiv:1210.4125, *Phys. Rev. D* **86** (2012) 117303, and "Koide's Z₃-symmetric parametrization, quark masses and mixings," arXiv:1301.4143, *Phys. Rev. D* **87** (2013) 077302, sharpens the Brannen Z₃ phase observation: δ_L = 2/9 is empirically indistinguishable from the exact value, and quark phases hint at δ_U = δ_L/3 = 2/27, δ_D = 2δ_L/3 = 4/27 — a lepton–quark sum rule δ_L + δ_ν = δ_U + δ_D. Z. Liang & Z. Sun, "A modified version of the Koide formula from flavor nonets in a scalar potential model and in a Yukawaon model," arXiv:2007.05878, gives modern flavor-nonet derivations.

11. **Empirical status (2026).** Using PDG 2024 values (m_τ = 1776.93 ± 0.09 MeV, dominated by the Belle II 2023 measurement of 1777.09 ± 0.08 (stat) ± 0.11 (sys) MeV from "the kinematic edge of the τ pseudomass distribution in the decay τ⁻ → π⁻π⁺π⁻ν_τ" using ~175 million e⁺e⁻ → τ⁺τ⁻ events at 190 fb⁻¹ at √s = 10.579 GeV, arXiv:2305.19116), Q deviates from 2/3 by ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻⁶, the most precise agreement in the relation's 44-year history. The Brannen phase δ = 0.222241 ± 0.000006 is consistent with 2/9 at < 1σ. The 6-fermion Kartavtsev formula and the heavy-quark formula remain at the few-percent level (G.-H. Gao & N. Li, "Explorations of two empirical formulae for fermion masses," arXiv:1512.06349, *Eur. Phys. J. C* **76** (2016) 360, find K_q ≈ 5% above 2/3, RG-stable from 1 GeV to 10¹² GeV). No quantitative null-model probability estimate has been peer-published, but two qualitative arguments cut against pure numerology: (i) the joint coincidence of Q = 2/3 *and* δ = 2/9 (Żenczykowski 2012); (ii) the relation has tracked m_τ refinements over 30+ years.

12. **Cross-sector "universal" Koide proposals.** Kartavtsev 2011 (universal 6-lepton); Cao 2012 (heavy-quark + lepton joint use); Li & Ma 2005 (quark–lepton complementarity k_l + k_d ≈ k_ν + k_u ≈ 2, from arXiv:hep-ph/0505028, *Phys. Lett. B* **609** (2005) 309); J.-M. Gerard, F. Goffinet, M. Herquet, "A New Look at an Old Mass Relation," hep-ph/0510289, *Phys. Lett. B* **633** (2006) 563, who argued that Q_f = 1 holds for extremely hierarchical spectra and Q_f = 1/3 for degenerate ones, so 2/3 is exactly halfway and corresponds to the Dirac-mass scale around 0.03 eV for the lightest neutrino; Goffinet's PhD thesis (Université catholique de Louvain, 2008) reformulates the relation without square roots; Z.-Q. Guo & B.-Q. Ma, hep-ph/0702288, connects Foot's geometric interpretation to the tribimaximal MNS matrix; W. Krolikowski, hep-ph/0509043, combines Koide with a second mass sum rule to over-determine the masses; Y.-C. Huang, S. T. Iqbal et al., arXiv:1601.00754, applies seesaw to predict left- and right-handed neutrino masses from Koide. None reaches the < 10⁻⁵ accuracy of the charged-lepton triple, supporting the view that Q = 2/3 is genuinely special there.

### D. Geometric and group-theoretic readings
- **Esposito & Santorelli 1995** (hep-ph/9603369, *Mod. Phys. Lett. A* **10** (1995) 3077): a geometric picture for fermion masses, RG-invariant below the electroweak scale; pre-Foot but with similar geometric flavor.
- **Kocik 2012** (arXiv:1201.2067, physics.gen-ph): three coplanar circles meeting pairwise at φ = arccos(2/3) ≈ 48.2°. Closest published precedent to a geometric mechanism. n-dim generalization given.
- **Guo & Ma 2007** (hep-ph/0702288): Foot's geometry connected to tribimaximal mixing.

### E. Theoretical mechanisms (attempts to derive Q = 2/3)
1. **Composite/preon original** (Koide 1982/1983): mᵢ ∝ gᵢ² with sum constraints Σgᵢ⁽⁸⁾ = 0 and Σ(gᵢ⁽⁸⁾)² = 3(g⁽¹⁾)² yield Koide. Quark/CKM side-predictions falsified.
2. **U(3)-family scalar potential** (Koide 1990 onward): scalar nonet Φ with VEV ⟨Φ⟩ ∝ diag(√mₑ, √m_μ, √m_τ); Koide emerges as the minimum of a Z₃-symmetric potential.
3. **Sumino family-gauge cancellation** (0812.2090, 0812.2103): U(3) gauge bosons at 10²–10³ TeV cancel QED logs for the Koide combination. Confronts the running-vs-pole problem head-on.
4. **Yukawaon models** (Koide & Nishiura 2008–2013, plus Liang & Sun 2007.05878): Yukawa as 3×3 scalar VEV; many variants (anomaly-free 1308.2129, U(3)×O(3), U(3)×S₃ 1202.5815).
5. **Discrete flavor** (Ma 2007, Koide A₄ 2007, Adler 1999, Kiselev 1998): Z₃ / A₄ / S₃ groups on triplet scalars produce mᵢ ∝ vᵢ², the structural prerequisite for Koide.
6. **Geometric / Descartes** (Kocik 2012): 3 circles, equal pairwise angle 48.2°.
7. **Z₃ Fourier circulant** (Brannen / Sheppeard 2006–): Koide is the DFT on three colors; the √2 prefactor is a constraint on the eigenvalue distribution.
8. **Speculative recent proposals (non-peer-reviewed)**: 2025 Zenodo "Cogito" S⁵-winding/AdS-CFT note (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19271373); Preprints.org "Phase Coherence" (May 2025, manuscript 202505.2156); E₈ + spectral geometry (Preprints.org 202511.0938, 2025); G₂ Casimir ratio for δ = 2/9 (ai.viXra 2602.0108v1, 2026); Brannen/Wilson 5-neutron / 3-proton-3-lepton coincidences (Azimuth 2021). These have not passed peer review and should be cited only as such.

### F. Empirical status under PDG 2024
- Using mₑ = 0.51099895000(15) MeV, m_μ = 105.6583755(23) MeV, m_τ = 1776.93(09) MeV: **Q = 0.66666446(508)**, deviation from 2/3 is ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻⁶, well within < 1σ.
- The Belle II 2023 measurement m_τ = 1777.09 ± 0.08(stat) ± 0.11(sys) MeV (arXiv:2305.19116) is the most precise to date and pulls the world average toward Koide's 1776.969 MeV prediction.
- The pole-mass relation Q = 2/3 holds at the 10⁻⁵ level; the running relation at M_Z deviates by K^run = (2/3)(1.00189), i.e. ≈ 0.2% (Xing–Zhang 2006), so any high-scale derivation requires either Sumino-style cancellation or an explanation of why pole masses are special.

## Recommendations
1. **Position the C₃ × Z₂ paper relative to four canonical anchors**: Koide 1982 (origin); Foot 1994 (45° geometry); Brannen 2006 (√mₖ ∝ 1 + √2 cos(δ + 2πk/3) circulant; δ = 2/9); Sumino 2008–2009 (running-vs-pole protection at 10²–10³ TeV). Any new "geometric mechanism based on C₃ × Z₂" must, at minimum, (a) reproduce Brannen's parametrization, (b) take a stance on Sumino's cancellation (does C₃ × Z₂ also protect the relation under RG, or does it apply at a fixed scale?), (c) explain why the relation does *not* hold for naive c-b-t or u-d-s without sign flips.
2. **Cite the Z₃ phase work** (Żenczykowski 2012/2013, *PRD* **86** (2012) 117303 and *PRD* **87** (2013) 077302; Brannen 2006) — this is where the C₃ structure is already explicit and where δ_L = 2/9 is most precisely formulated. The C₃ × Z₂ proposal will be evaluated against whether Z₂ explains the sign flip seen in (i) Brannen's neutrino formula (−√m₁), (ii) Rivero's s-c-b formula (−√mₛ).
3. **Distinguish your mechanism from Kocik's Descartes reading** explicitly: Kocik provides geometry but not dynamics. A C₃ × Z₂ mechanism with field content and a Lagrangian would be a clear advance.
4. **Address the published-vs-arXiv-only landscape honestly.** Foot 1994, Rivero–Gsponer 2005, Kocik 2012, Kartavtsev 2011, Rivero 2011, Brannen 2006 are arXiv-/website-only. The published peer-reviewed mechanism papers are Koide's, Sumino's, Xing–Zhang, Li–Ma, Cao, Rodejohann–Zhang, Ma 2007, Żenczykowski, Liang–Sun, Gerard–Goffinet–Herquet.
5. **Use PDG 2024 values** (m_τ = 1776.93(09) MeV) — cite the Belle II 2023 measurement explicitly as the precision update.
6. **Benchmarks that would change the verdict**: (i) if a future tau-mass measurement at < 0.05 MeV precision moves Q outside [2/3 − 10⁻⁵, 2/3 + 10⁻⁵], the relation is dead and no mechanism is needed; (ii) if neutrino-mass cosmology fixes m_lightest to ≳ 10⁻³ eV, Brannen's ν-Koide and Cao's predictions become directly testable; (iii) if a 100–1000 TeV family-gauge boson is detected (Sumino's signature), that program becomes the leading candidate and any C₃ × Z₂ proposal must be embedded in or consistent with it.

## Caveats
- **INSPIRE-HEP citation counts could not be retrieved directly** in this review due to JS-rendered pages and API restrictions; before final submission, every citation count should be checked at inspirehep.net/literature for the exact INSPIRE record (e.g. record 371545 for Foot 1994). Semantic Scholar shows Rivero–Gsponer hep-ph/0505220 is well-cited (corpus ID 16583689) but live counts were not verified here.
- **Brannen's "first publication" date is fuzzy.** The δ = 2/9 phase result is dated by the timestamp on brannenworks.com/MASSES.pdf (April 2006) and MASSES2.pdf (May 2006) plus the 2006 BAPS abstract. He had a brief arXiv listing that was withdrawn by moderators. Citation as "Brannen, Bull. Am. Phys. Soc. (2006)" or via Koide's hep-ph/0605074, which quotes him as "[Brannen]," is the cleanest peer-adjacent venue.
- **Sheppeard's contributions are mostly on viXra.** Citing viXra in a peer-reviewed paper is acceptable but risks reviewer pushback; prefer Brannen's papers where Sheppeard is acknowledged, or the Brannen–Sheppeard arXiv:0901.1687 (Colored Preons).
- **The Sumino mechanism is contested**: physics-forums and other commentary (e.g. the "What is new with Koide sum rules?" thread) argues that requiring Q = 2/3 at a high scale is itself physically unmotivated, and that no evidence for suppression of QED radiative corrections between mₑ and m_W exists. Li & Ma's 2006 RG-stability claim sits in tension with Xing–Zhang's K^run(M_Z) = (2/3)(1.00189); a careful reader should look at both.
- **"Universal" 6-fermion or all-12-fermion Koide formulas fail empirically** at the few-percent level (Gao & Li 2016); this argues the charged-lepton triple is genuinely privileged, supporting C₃ × Z₂ acting specifically there.
- **Post-2024 "derivations" on Zenodo and preprints.org** (Phase Coherence; Casimir; AdS/CFT cogito; E₈ spectral) are non-peer-reviewed and should be flagged as such if cited at all.
- **No string-theory/brane construction was located that *derives* Koide.** D-brane and orbifold flavor models reproduce some related sum rules but none has been argued to imply Q = 2/3 at the 10⁻⁵ precision. Tanimoto–Watari–Yanagida (hep-ph/9904338) on broken O(3)_L × O(3)_R democratic mass matrices is the closest stringy/SUSY environment.
- **Rivero–Gsponer's paper has often been credited (e.g. on Wikipedia)** with first noting the c-b-t coincidence, but the Wikipedia article is explicit that the *first published mention of the c-b-t Koide tuple* is Cao 2012 (the Rodejohann–Zhang preprint v1 was the first arXiv mention; the published version stripped it; Rivero's 1111.7232 is concurrent and unpublished). When citing, use the dual citation Rodejohann–Zhang v1 / Cao 2012 to avoid priority disputes.
