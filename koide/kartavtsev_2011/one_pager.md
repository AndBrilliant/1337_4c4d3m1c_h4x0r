# Kartavtsev 2011 — Six-quark democratic sum

## Reference
A. Kartavtsev, *A remark on the Koide relation for quarks*, **arXiv:1111.0480** (Nov 2011). Never journal-published.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §3 / §C.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Proposes the **six-quark democratic sum** K_q = (Σ_q m_q)/(Σ_q √m_q)² ≈ 2/3 over all six quarks (u, d, s, c, b, t) and similarly K_l for all six leptons. Finds the relation insensitive to RG running. The 6-quark formula is *less* precise than the charged-lepton 3-formula (~5% above 2/3 per Gao-Li 2016), but achieves the universality the charged-lepton-specific relation lacks.

## Sections / contents
- Definition of K_q for the 6-quark sum.
- Empirical fit: K_q ≈ 2/3 to ~5% precision.
- RG-stability check.
- Parallel definition of K_l for 6 leptons.
- Implications for any "universal Koide" mechanism.
