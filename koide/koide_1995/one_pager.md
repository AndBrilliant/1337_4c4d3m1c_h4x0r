# Koide 1995 — U(3) family nonet Higgs

## Reference

Koide literature review research report, §A. Cited as the U(3)-nonet revival.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Develops the U(3)-family nonet Higgs framework introduced in Koide 1990 (Mod. Phys. Lett. A 5 2319), including new-physics implications. Treats the nonet of Higgs scalars carrying flavor indices as the source both of the Koide relation and of family-violating processes that could be searched for experimentally.

## Sections / contents

- Scalar potential and VEV alignment.
- Koide relation as the consequence of the symmetric minimum.
- New-physics signatures (FCNC, family-violating processes).
