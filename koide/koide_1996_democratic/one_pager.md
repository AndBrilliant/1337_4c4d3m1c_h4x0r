# Koide 1996 — Democratic seesaw quark mass matrix related to charged-lepton masses

## Reference
Y. Koide, *A democratic seesaw quark mass matrix related to the charged lepton masses*, **arXiv:hep-ph/9602303** (1996).

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Refines the democratic-seesaw quark mass matrix to **directly relate it to the charged-lepton masses** that satisfy the Koide relation. The setup uses the same √m structure for both sectors, with the seesaw integrating out heavy partners producing the observed quark hierarchy. Counted as a key cross-sector linkage paper in Koide's program.

## Sections / contents
- Democratic seesaw quark mass matrix.
- Cross-sector linkage to charged-lepton √m structure.
- Predictions for CKM mixing.
- Implications for the up- and down-quark mass spectra.
