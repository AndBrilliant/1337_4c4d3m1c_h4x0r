# Koide 2000 — Cyclic-permutation-invariant mass matrices

## Reference
Y. Koide, *Quark and lepton mass matrices with a cyclic permutation invariant form*, **arXiv:hep-ph/0005137** (2000).

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §A.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Constructs **cyclic-permutation-invariant (Z₃-cyclic) mass matrices** for both quarks and leptons. The Z₃ cyclic structure is the abelian skeleton underneath the larger S₃ democratic family symmetry; this is the form that later Brannen/Sheppeard/Żenczykowski work elevated to a **Z₃-Fourier / circulant** reading. Important precursor to the Brannen 2006 cosine parametrization and to the Żenczykowski 2012/2013 PRD analysis.

## Sections / contents
- Z₃-cyclic mass-matrix ansatz.
- Application to charged leptons (deriving Koide).
- Application to quarks (cross-sector predictions).
- Connection to the broader S₃ democratic framework.
