## Beyond-abstract summary
Companion to PRD 78 093006 (arXiv:0809.2449). Develops the **yukawaon framework** further: the 3×3 scalar Yukawa-as-VEV machinery, with refinements to the VEV-alignment scalar potential and to the family-symmetry choice. Provides additional cross-sector predictions and explores variants of the symmetric scalar potential.

## Sections / contents
- Refined yukawaon scalar potential.
- VEV-alignment mechanism.
- Family-symmetry variants.
- Cross-sector predictions for quark-mass matrices.
- Connection to existing democratic-seesaw work (Koide 1999 and earlier).
