The formula A will be satisfied only masses switched off all interactions except for U(3) family interactions. In this talk, the following views are given: (i) What direction of flavor physics research is suggested by the formula A; (ii) How the Sumino model is misunderstood by people and what we should learn from his model; (iii) What is strategy of my recent work, U(3)$\times$U(3)$&#39;$ model.
