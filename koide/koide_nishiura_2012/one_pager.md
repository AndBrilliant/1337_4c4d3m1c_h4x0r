# Koide & Nishiura 2012 — Yukawaon + U(3)×S₃

## Reference

## Beyond-abstract summary
Yukawaon model with a combined **U(3) × S₃ family symmetry**. The S₃ subgroup ensures the democratic structure that produces Koide at tree level, while U(3) provides the family-gauge-symmetry context that Sumino (2008) used to protect the relation against radiative corrections.

- Scalar potential and VEV alignment.
- Tree-level Koide derivation.
- Family-gauge-boson radiative protection.
- Cross-sector predictions.
