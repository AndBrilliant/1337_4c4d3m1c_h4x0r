# Koide & Yamashita 2012 — Inverted-hierarchy family gauge bosons

## Reference
Y. Koide and T. Yamashita, **arXiv:1203.2028** → ***Phys. Lett. B* **711** (2012) 384**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §A. Identified as the **SUSY-compatible variant of Sumino**.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Critical paper. Constructs a **SUSY-compatible variant of the Sumino mechanism** with **inverted hierarchy** for the family gauge bosons: m(A_i^i) ∝ 1/√m_ei. The standard Sumino mechanism requires non-SUSY because the radiative cancellation between QED logs and family-gauge-boson logs cannot be straightforwardly applied in SUSY (the non-renormalization theorem). Koide & Yamashita show that an **inverted** family-gauge-boson hierarchy resolves this, making Koide derivation compatible with low-energy SUSY.
