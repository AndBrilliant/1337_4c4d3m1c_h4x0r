# Foot 1994 — Koide's relation as a $C_3$ projection

## Reference
R. Foot, *A note on Koide's lepton mass relation*, **arXiv:hep-ph/9402242** (7 Feb 1994); McGill/94-9, Department of Physics, McGill University. Three-page note; no journal publication recorded.

## Source
- Research report §7 (Koide formula in CM contexts), tier ★.

## Beyond-abstract summary
A two-equation note observing that Koide's relation $Q=2/3$ is equivalent to the statement that the vector $(\sqrt{m_e},\sqrt{m_\mu},\sqrt{m_\tau})$ makes an angle $\theta = \pi/4$ with the symmetric diagonal $(1,1,1)$. Explicitly, $\cos\theta = (\sqrt{m_e}+\sqrt{m_\mu}+\sqrt{m_\tau})/\sqrt{3(m_e+m_\mu+m_\tau)}$, so $\cos\theta = 1/\sqrt{2}$ reproduces Koide. The constraint $\cos\theta > 1/\sqrt{3}$ (i.e. $\theta < 54.7^\circ$) follows from at least one mass being nonzero. Putting in PDG values gives $\theta = 45.000^\circ \pm 0.001^\circ$. Replacing $2/3$ with $3/4$ or $1/2$ in Koide's relation gives $\theta = 48.189^\circ$ or $35.264^\circ$ — neither is special; $\theta = \pi/4$ is. Foot notes that this "geometrical formulation" may be useful for deriving the relation from an underlying theory but offers no mechanism.

## Use in the F-identity manuscript
Cited as `Foot1994` in Sec. 2.1 of the F-identity paper for the "cone" interpretation: with $\delta=2/9$, the Brannen parametrization places $(\sqrt{m_e},\sqrt{m_\mu},\sqrt{m_\tau})$ on the cone $\cos\theta=1/\sqrt{2}$ around the symmetric axis. Foot establishes that the $\pi/4$ angle is the geometric content of $Q=2/3$; the F-identity paper builds the $C_3 \times Z_2$ phase-cancellation structure *on* that cone, with $\delta$ fixing the position around the axis. Foot's note is therefore prior art for the cone, not for the cancellation.
