# Li & Ma 2006 — Energy scale independence of Koide's relation

## Reference
N. Li, B.-Q. Ma, *Energy scale independence of Koide's relation for quark and lepton masses*, **Phys. Rev. D 73 (2006) 013009**; **arXiv:hep-ph/0601031**.

## Source
§7 (Koide formula), tier ✦ — RG-stability analysis of Koide's relation.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Performs one-loop RG running of the Koide deviation parameter $\Delta K \equiv 3K/2 - 1$ for the charged-lepton, up-quark, and down-quark sectors of the Standard Model. Shows that the deviation $\Delta K$ is *RG-stable* to within $\sim 10^{-3}$–$10^{-2}$ across the range from $m_t$ down to $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$ — i.e. the Koide relation, if exact at one scale, is approximately exact at all scales. This contrasts with the running of individual masses (which evolve by factors of 2–10 across this range) and provides the empirical bridge between "Koide for pole masses" and "Koide as a scale-free relation."

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Cited (with Leutwyler 1996) as the canonical reference for "Koide is essentially RG-invariant," supporting the F-identity manuscript's comparison of single-scale theoretical predictions to PDG/FLAG running masses.
