## Beyond-abstract summary
Builds a compact-cycle effective theory for the charged-lepton sector. The internal "family" coordinate is an angle $\varphi \sim \varphi + 2\pi$ on a circle; the two lowest antiperiodic modes $\xi_\pm(\varphi) = e^{\pm i\varphi/2}$ span an internal sign space whose symmetric square is the minimal three-harmonic family space $\{e^{i\varphi}, 1, e^{-i\varphi}\}$. A neutral symmetric sign-bilinear order parameter $Q_{AB}$, parametrized in branch-real form $Q = \begin{pmatrix} d & s/\sqrt 2 \\ s/\sqrt 2 & d^* \end{pmatrix}$ with $s\in\mathbb{R}$, $d=|d|e^{i\theta}$, generates the family amplitude $Z(\varphi) = s + 2|d|\cos(\varphi+\theta)$; the masses are $m_a \propto |Z(2\pi a/3)|^2$. Coherence ($\det Q = 0$, i.e. $Q$ is the symmetric square of one spinor) gives $s^2 = 2|d|^2$, which is precisely the Koide 45° cone after sampling at three equally spaced points. The remaining cone orientation $\theta_\ell$ is fixed by treating one $C_3$ family shift as transport on the full circle: integrating out the higher Fourier harmonics with the inverse-Laplacian Green function dresses one elementary shift link by a Berry phase $\gamma_{C_3} = 2/9$, and the determinant vertex composes three such links into $\theta_\ell = -2/9$. With $m_e, m_\mu$ as input, the model predicts $m_\tau = 1776.97$ MeV. Strict-$\theta = -2/9$ ratio agrees with experiment to ~$10^{-5}$; the residual is naturally interpreted as the size of compact-cycle / higher-harmonic corrections.
