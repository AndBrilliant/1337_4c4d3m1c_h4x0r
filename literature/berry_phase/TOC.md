# berry_phase/ — Table of Contents

> §2 of the source report: *Berry phase in mass/spectrum determination.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: §2.1 invokes Shulga's compact-cycle Berry-phase derivation of $\delta = 2/9$. This subfolder gathers CM precedents in which a Berry phase fixes a numerical observable.

## Tier 1 (★ — master CM Berry-phase review)

- [Xiao, Chang & Niu 2010](xiao_chang_niu_2010/one_pager.md) — *Berry phase effects on electronic properties,* RMP 82 (2010) 1959. Canonical reference showing Berry phases determine Hall conductance, polarization, orbital magnetization, and wavepacket effective masses.

## Tier 1 (★ — prototype: Berry → quantized integer)

- [TKNN 1982](tknn_1982/one_pager.md) — Thouless, Kohmoto, Nightingale, den Nijs, *Quantized Hall conductance in a 2D periodic potential,* PRL 49 (1982) 405. First Chern number fixes σ_xy.

## Tier 1 (★ — experimental: Berry → mass)

- [Jauregui et al. 2015](jauregui_2015/one_pager.md) — *Gate-tunable relativistic mass and Berry's phase in TI nanoribbons,* Sci. Rep. 5 (2015) 8452. Concrete experimental "Berry phase fixes a Dirac mass."

## Tier 1 (★ — Z₂-valued Berry phase)

- [Zak 1989](zak_1989/one_pager.md) — *Berry's phase for energy bands in solids,* PRL 62 (1989) 2747. Prototype Z₂-valued Berry phase (polarization mod e).

## Supporting (✦)

- [Atala et al. 2013](atala_2013/one_pager.md) — *Direct measurement of the Zak phase,* Nat. Phys. 9 (2013) 795. Experimental π Zak phase in SSH chain.
- [Tse & MacDonald 2010](tse_macdonald_2010/one_pager.md) — *Giant magneto-optical Kerr / universal Faraday effect in TI thin films,* PRL 105 (2010) 057401. Berry-phase quantization in units of α.
