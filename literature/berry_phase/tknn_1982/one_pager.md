# TKNN 1982 — Quantized Hall conductance in a 2D periodic potential

D. J. Thouless, M. Kohmoto, M. P. Nightingale, M. den Nijs, *Quantized Hall conductance in a two-dimensional periodic potential*, **Phys. Rev. Lett. 49 (1982) 405–408**. (No arXiv; pre-arXiv era.)

§2 (Berry phase in mass/spectrum determination), tier ★ — the original Berry-phase / first-Chern-number paper, prototype of "Berry-curvature integral fixes a quantized observable."

## Beyond-abstract summary
The foundational paper showing that for a 2D electron gas in a periodic potential and a perpendicular magnetic field, the quantized Hall conductivity is $\sigma_{xy} = (e^2/h) \cdot \nu$, where $\nu$ is an integer given by an integral of the Berry curvature over the magnetic Brillouin zone — the first Chern number of the $U(1)$ Bloch bundle over $T^2$. This established that a topological invariant from differential geometry determines an exactly quantized physical observable, and it remains the prototype example of "Berry phase fixes a numerical parameter."

## Use in F-identity manuscript
The foundational reference for the entire program of "geometric phases fix observables." Cited together with Xiao–Chang–Niu 2010 and Shulga 2026 when establishing the CM precedent for the Berry-phase determination of $\delta = 2/9$ in the F-identity construction.
