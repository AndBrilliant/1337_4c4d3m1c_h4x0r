## Beyond-abstract summary
Effective continuum treatment of graphene on slightly lattice-mismatched substrates (hBN or Ir(111)). The 1.8% lattice mismatch creates a Moiré superstructure whose substrate-induced potential prevents the band-gap opening at the Dirac point predicted for perfectly matched graphene, leaving a renormalized Dirac dispersion with **threefold (trigonal) warping**. A second set of massless Dirac fermions emerges at the supercell-BZ corners with collinear group velocity $v_F^R = v_F^0/2$ — exactly half the pristine-graphene velocity. The factor of 1/2 is forced by the $C_3$-protected emergent degeneracy structure, not fitted.
