> §9 of the source report: *Cross-domain reviews (HEP ↔ CM).*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: §1 of the paper positions the construction as cross-domain (HEP→CM). Reviewers will want to know the established review literature on CM↔HEP analogy; this subfolder collects it.

## Tier 1 (★ — book-length cross-domain reference)

- [Volovik 2003 book](volovik_2003_book/one_pager.md) — *The Universe in a Helium Droplet,* Oxford International Series of Monographs on Physics 117 (Oxford UP, 2003). Comprehensive treatment of CM↔HEP analogies (chiral fermions, Lorentz invariance, gauge fields, topology) via ³He.

## Tier 1 (★ — RMP TI review with HEP dictionary)

- [Qi & Zhang 2011](qi_zhang_2011/one_pager.md) — *Topological insulators and superconductors,* RMP 83 (2011) 1057. Explicit Dirac/Majorana ↔ CM-band-structure dictionary; θ-term axion field theory.

## Tier 1 (★ — RMP TI colloquium)

- [Hasan & Kane 2010](hasan_kane_2010/one_pager.md) — *Colloquium: Topological insulators,* RMP 82 (2010) 3045. Standard cross-domain review; Z₂, Berry phases, Dirac mass terms.

## Tier 1 (★ — cross-listed with §4)

- *(cross-ref)* Schnyder, Ryu, Furusaki & Ludwig 2008 — see `../z2_particle_hole/schnyder_2008/`. The tenfold-way classification doubles as a CM↔HEP review for single-particle Hamiltonians.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Tsuji, Danshita & Tsuchiya 2023](tsuji_danshita_tsuchiya_2023/one_pager.md) — *Higgs and Nambu–Goldstone modes in CM,* arXiv:2310.17148. Modern cross-domain framing of Higgs/Goldstone modes.
