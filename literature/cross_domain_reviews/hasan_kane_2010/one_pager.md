## Beyond-abstract summary
The pedagogical RMP Colloquium on topological insulators. Reviews the $Z_2$ Kane–Mele invariant, the 3D extension, the HgTe and Bi₂Se₃ / Bi₂Te₃ / Bi₁₋ₓSbₓ material families, and the Dirac-mass interpretation of the surface states. Strongest single citation for "Dirac fermions / mass terms / Berry phases in a CM context, narrated in HEP language." The single most-readable entry point to the TI field as of 2010.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Standard cross-domain review citation. Reader-friendly entry point for HEP physicists unfamiliar with the CM topological-insulator literature.
