## Beyond-abstract summary
The companion RMP review (to Hasan–Kane 2010) on topological insulators and superconductors, with heavier emphasis on the HEP↔CM correspondence. Develops the topological-field-theory description: 3D topological insulators are characterized by a $\theta$-term in the electromagnetic action with $\theta = \pi$ for the topological phase and $\theta = 0$ for the trivial phase — exactly the axion electrodynamics framework from HEP. Covers Dirac/Majorana fermion classifications, the BdG ↔ TR-invariant SC mapping, and the θ-term's transformation under time reversal. Strongest single CM-side reference for HEP-language analogies.
