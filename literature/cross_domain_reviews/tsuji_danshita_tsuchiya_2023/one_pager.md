# Tsuji, Danshita & Tsuchiya 2023 — Higgs and Nambu–Goldstone modes in condensed matter

## Reference
N. Tsuji, I. Danshita, S. Tsuchiya, *Higgs and Nambu–Goldstone modes in condensed matter physics*, **arXiv:2310.17148** (October 2023).

## Source
§9 (Cross-domain HEP↔CM reviews), tier ✦ — modern cross-domain review framing Higgs/Goldstone modes as objects shared across CM and HEP.

## Beyond-abstract summary
A modern (2023) review explicitly framing Higgs and Nambu–Goldstone modes as cross-domain objects with shared mass-formula structures. Discusses: (i) the Higgs mode in superconductors / cold atoms / antiferromagnets and its analogy to the SM Higgs; (ii) Nambu–Goldstone bosons in symmetry-broken CM systems and their HEP counterparts; (iii) Nambu sum rules and mass relations; (iv) experimental observation channels in pump–probe spectroscopy. Useful as a contemporary entry point to the cross-domain mass-formula literature, complementing the 1960s–70s NJL foundational work and Volovik's monograph.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Modern citation for the position that mass-formula transport between CM and HEP is active and productive in the 2020s, not a historical analogy. Useful in the F-identity Discussion when motivating the cross-domain framing.
