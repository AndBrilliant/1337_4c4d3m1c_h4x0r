## Beyond-abstract summary
A short popular-science gateway article describing the Apollonian gasket, the Descartes/Soddy circle theorem, and applications to 2D foam (with bubble-size distribution following a power law $N(r) \propto r^{-\delta}$, $\delta \approx 1.3$) and to number-theoretic patterns in integer bends. Useful as a single-citation entry point for readers unfamiliar with the gasket.
