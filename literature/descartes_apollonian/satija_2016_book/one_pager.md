# Satija 2016 (book) — Butterfly in the Quantum World

## Reference
I. I. Satija, with contributions by D. R. Hofstadter, **Butterfly in the Quantum World: The Story of the Most Fascinating Quantum Fractal**, IOP Concise Physics / Morgan & Claypool (San Rafael, 2016), ISBN 978-1-6817-4053-9.

## Source
§1 (Descartes/Apollonian in CM), tier ★ — book-length CM treatment, companion to Satija (2016) EPJ ST.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Single-volume CM-side treatment of the Hofstadter butterfly, integer Apollonian gaskets, Farey trees, Pythagorean-triple trees, and the integer quantum Hall effect. The book extends the EPJ ST companion paper (arXiv:1606.09119) with pedagogical context, historical material from Hofstadter's contributions, and worked examples of the butterfly↔gasket mapping. Ideal as a single CM-textbook reference for the integer Apollonian / Soddy-circle framework relevant to the F-identity construction.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Book-length companion reference; cited in the Discussion as the textbook treatment of CM Apollonian/butterfly physics — gives a referee an extended treatment of the κ=√m / integer-bend identification beyond the EPJ ST paper.
