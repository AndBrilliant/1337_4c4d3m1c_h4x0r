> §10 of the source report: *Effective field theory bridging CM and HEP.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: the closing-discussion claim is that the construction provides "geometric constraints — closed-form relations among masses — that any theory of the Yukawa sector would need to reproduce." This subfolder collects the EFT-style precedents for HEP↔CM mass-relation transport.

## Tier 1 (★ — explicit cross-domain mass relation)

- [Volovik 2014](volovik_2014/one_pager.md) — *Higgs bosons in particle physics and in condensed matter,* J. Low Temp. Phys. 175 (2014) 486 [arXiv:1305.7219]. Top-quark condensation Nambu sum rule 2M₁² + 2M₂² = 4M_t² — explicit HEP-style EFT generating mass relations.

## Tier 1 (★ — Higgs ↔ Leggett mode dictionary)

- [Volovik & Zubkov 2014](volovik_zubkov_2014/one_pager.md) — arXiv:1410.7097. Higgs and Leggett modes as pseudo-Nambu–Goldstone bosons of the same EFT structure.

## Tier 1 (★ — historical: CM → HEP import)

- [Nambu & Jona-Lasinio 1961](nambu_jona_lasinio_1961/one_pager.md) — *Dynamical model of elementary particles based on analogy with superconductivity. I & II,* Phys. Rev. 122 (1961) 345 and 124 (1961) 246. Imported BCS gap-equation mass generation into HEP — historical anchor of the entire bridge.

## Tier 1 (★ — HEP → CM RG)

- [Shankar 1994](shankar_1994/one_pager.md) — *RG approach to interacting fermions,* RMP 66 (1994) 129. Canonical application of Wilsonian EFT to CM Fermi liquids.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Polchinski 1992](polchinski_1992/one_pager.md) — *Effective field theory and the Fermi surface,* TASI lectures [arXiv:hep-th/9210046]. Textbook HEP-style EFT power counting applied to CM; BCS as RG-relevant.
