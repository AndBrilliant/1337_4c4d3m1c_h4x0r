## Beyond-abstract summary
The two-part 1961 foundational paper(s) where Nambu and Jona-Lasinio model elementary-particle mass generation in direct analogy with the BCS theory of superconductivity. The mechanism: a four-fermion interaction $G(\bar\psi\psi)^2 + G(\bar\psi i\gamma_5\psi)^2$ generates a fermion condensate $\langle\bar\psi\psi\rangle \neq 0$ via the gap equation, dynamically breaking chiral symmetry and producing a fermion mass $m = -2G\langle\bar\psi\psi\rangle$. The work introduces three foundational concepts simultaneously: (i) spontaneous chiral symmetry breaking in HEP, (ii) the Nambu–Goldstone boson (the pion as the would-be Goldstone mode), and (iii) the CM-to-HEP analogy as a productive research direction. The historical anchor of the entire HEP↔CM EFT bridge.
