# Shankar 1994 — Renormalization-group approach to interacting fermions

## Reference

## Source
§10 (EFT bridge), tier ★ — the canonical application of Wilsonian EFT/RG to CM Fermi liquids.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The standard RMP review showing how Wilsonian renormalization-group / effective-field-theory methods (developed for HEP) apply rigorously to CM Fermi liquids. The framework: integrating out high-momentum modes near a Fermi surface yields a flow in coupling-constant space; the relevant operators are the BCS pairing channel and the forward-scattering Landau parameters; everything else is irrelevant. The work makes precise the analogy "CM Fermi-liquid theory ↔ HEP EFT," establishing that RG methods cross domains seamlessly. The reference for "Wilsonian EFT applied to CM."

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Standard reference for "HEP EFT methods are at home in CM." Cited (with Polchinski 1992) when establishing the bidirectional CM↔HEP EFT transport that underlies the F-identity manuscript's cross-domain claim.
