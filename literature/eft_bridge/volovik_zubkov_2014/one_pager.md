## Beyond-abstract summary
Establishes a precise dictionary in which: the 125 GeV Higgs particle in top-quark-condensation models, and the Leggett-frequency scalar excitation in superfluid ³He-B, are *both* pseudo-Nambu–Goldstone bosons of the same effective Lagrangian structure. The dictionary makes the cross-domain transport of mass-relation results rigorous rather than merely analogical. The paper is the cleanest single demonstration that Nambu–Goldstone mass generation operates identically in both CM (³He-B) and HEP (top-quark-condensation Higgs) contexts.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Companion to Volovik 2014 (J. Low Temp. Phys.). Establishes that the CM-HEP correspondence is structural (shared EFT Lagrangian) rather than aesthetic.
