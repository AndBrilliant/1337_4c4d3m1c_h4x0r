# Hashin & Shtrikman 1963 — Variational bounds on elastic moduli

## Reference
Z. Hashin, S. Shtrikman, *A variational approach to the theory of the elastic behaviour of multiphase materials*, **J. Mech. Phys. Solids 11 (1963) 127–140**. No arXiv (1963).

## Source
§5 (Geometric means in EMT), tier ★ — canonical variational bounds on effective elastic moduli.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Establishes the tightest variational bounds on the effective bulk and shear moduli of an isotropic two-phase composite that can be expressed in terms of constituent moduli and volume fractions alone. The "Hashin–Shtrikman bounds" are achieved by specific microgeometries (the HS coated-sphere assemblage). The mathematical framework derives from the Hashin–Shtrikman variational principle — a modification of the standard elastic-energy variational principle that allows the trial-field choice to be a stress-polarization rather than a displacement. The bounds are universal: no random two-phase composite can violate them, regardless of microstructure.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Companion citation to Dykhne 1971 / Matheron 1967 for the CM tradition of *exact analytic relations* in effective-medium theory. The HS bounds are not themselves geometric means (they're algebraically more complex), but they establish that "constituent-property-only" exact statements about effective behavior are a routine and rigorous CM enterprise — supporting the position that the F-identity manuscript's predictions ($m_s = \alpha_K^2\,\mu_\star$, etc.) belong to a recognized analytic-anchor tradition.
