# Milton 2002 — The Theory of Composites

## Reference
G. W. Milton, **The Theory of Composites**, Cambridge Monographs on Applied and Computational Mathematics **6** (Cambridge University Press, 2002), 719 pp., ISBN 0-521-78125-6.

## Source
§5 (Geometric means in EMT), tier ✦ — standard modern monograph.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The definitive 700-page modern reference on effective-medium theory and composite materials. Covers the full mathematical and physical structure: bounds (Voigt–Reuss, Hashin–Shtrikman, Wiener), exact relations (Matheron–Dykhne, Schulgasser's theorem, Keller's theorem, Mendelson's theorem), homogenization, numerical methods, and applications to elastic, conductive, dielectric, magnetic, and other transport-coefficient problems. Includes explicit treatment of the 2D geometric-mean exact relations and the conditions under which they hold.
