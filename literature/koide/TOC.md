## A. Yoshio Koide — original 1982/83 papers

## B. Koide — S₃ / U(3) / democratic seesaw program (1989–2005)

- [Koide 1997 review](koide_1997_review/one_pager.md) — hep-ph/9705239, democratic seesaw and new physics.

- [Haba et al. 2000](haba_2000/one_pager.md) — hep-ph/0005064, democratic-matrix radiative stability for leptons.
