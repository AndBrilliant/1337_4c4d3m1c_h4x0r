## Beyond-abstract summary
The **most precise single tau-mass measurement to date.** Uses ~175 million e⁺e⁻ → τ⁺τ⁻ events at √s = 10.579 GeV from 190 fb⁻¹ at SuperKEKB; the measurement comes from the kinematic edge of the τ pseudomass distribution in the decay τ⁻ → π⁻π⁺π⁻ν_τ. Pulls the world average toward Koide's original 1776.969 MeV prediction. Combined with PDG 2024, gives **Q = 0.66666446(508)**, deviation from 2/3 ≈ **2.2 × 10⁻⁶** — the most precise agreement in the relation's 44-year history.
