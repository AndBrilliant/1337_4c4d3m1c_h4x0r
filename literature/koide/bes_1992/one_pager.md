# BES 1992 — Tau-mass vindication of Koide's 1982 prediction

## Reference

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §J. Identified as the **vindication of Koide's 1982 m_τ prediction**.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The historic measurement that vindicated Koide's 1982 prediction of m_τ ≈ 1.777 GeV. At the time of Koide's original paper, the world-average tau mass was 1.7842 GeV — more than 2σ above Koide's prediction. The BES 1992 measurement at BEPC gave m_τ = 1776.9 +0.4/−0.5 ± 0.2 MeV, almost exactly on Koide's prediction, and the world average has migrated to that value ever since. Still cited as the experimental anchor of the relation's empirical credibility.
