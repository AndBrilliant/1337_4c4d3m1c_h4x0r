# Cao 2012 — First journal-published heavy-quark Koide

## Reference
F. G. Cao, *Neutrino masses from lepton and quark mass relations and neutrino oscillations*, **arXiv:1205.4068** → ***Phys. Rev. D* **85** (2012) 113003**, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.85.113003.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §3 / §C / §H.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The **first journal-published** statement of the heavy-quark Koide relation Q_heavy = (m_c+m_b+m_t)/(√m_c+√m_b+√m_t)² ≈ 0.669 (Rodejohann-Zhang 2011 had this in v1 but stripped it in print). Inverts Koide-like cross-sector relations to predict absolute neutrino masses: m_1 = 0.21⁺¹·⁷⁰₋₀.₂₁ × 10⁻⁴ eV (very large uncertainty), m_2 = (8.7 ± 0.1) × 10⁻³ eV, m_3 = (4.9 ± 0.1) × 10⁻² eV with **normal hierarchy strongly preferred**.
