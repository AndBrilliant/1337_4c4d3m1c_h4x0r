## Beyond-abstract summary
Detailed RG-stability analysis of two empirical fermion-mass formulae including the Koide relation and Kartavtsev's universal-quark formula. Shows that **K_q is RG-stable from 1 GeV to 10¹² GeV** but is always **~5% above 2/3** — confirming that the universal-quark Koide is structurally different from the (exact) charged-lepton case. Important constraint on cross-sector or "universal Koide" mechanisms.

## Sections / contents
- Recap of charged-lepton Koide and Kartavtsev's universal K_q.
- RG running across 1 GeV–10¹² GeV.
- K_q stable but ~5% above 2/3 across the entire range.
- Implications for "universal" Koide proposals.
- Implicit support for the view that charged-lepton Koide is genuinely special.
