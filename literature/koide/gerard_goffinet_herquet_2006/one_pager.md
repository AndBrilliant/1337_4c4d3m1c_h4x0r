## Beyond-abstract summary
Notable extension of the Koide relation framework to all four sectors. Argues that the Koide ratio Q_f satisfies **Q_f = 1 for extremely hierarchical spectra** and **Q_f = 1/3 for degenerate spectra**, so **Q_f = 2/3 sits exactly halfway** between these extremes. For the neutrino sector, this corresponds to a Dirac-mass scale around **0.03 eV for the lightest neutrino** and **rules out maximal atmospheric mixing** in their framework. Goffinet's 2008 PhD thesis (UCLouvain) developed this further into the "pseudo-mass" cubic reformulation.

## Sections / contents
- Bounds Q_f ∈ [1/3, 1] for any 3-component fermion sector.
- Q_f = 1 (extreme hierarchy) and Q_f = 1/3 (degenerate) limits.
- Q_f = 2/3 as the midpoint.
- Application to charged leptons, neutrinos, up- and down-quarks.
- Prediction: lightest neutrino Dirac mass ≈ 0.03 eV.
- Implication: rules out maximal atmospheric mixing.
