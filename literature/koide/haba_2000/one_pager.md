# Haba et al. 2000 — Democratic-matrix radiative stability for leptons

## Reference
N. Haba et al., **arXiv:hep-ph/0005064** (2000).

## Source
Koide-comprehensive research report, §5 (Discrete-flavor / scalar-potential derivations).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Studies the **radiative stability of democratic-matrix patterns** in the lepton sector. The radiative-correction problem is the key obstacle to deriving Koide at tree level in S₃/democratic models, and Haba et al. provide a partial answer for the lepton sector — addressing the kind of issue Sumino's family-gauge mechanism (2008) later tackled head-on.

## Sections / contents
- Democratic-matrix flavor structure for leptons.
- One-loop radiative corrections.
- Stability / instability of Koide-type sum rules under running.
- Implications for high-scale vs. low-scale Koide validity.
