# Huang, Iqbal et al. 2016 — Seesaw + Koide for left- and right-handed neutrino masses

## Reference

## Beyond-abstract summary
Applies the **seesaw mechanism** in conjunction with Koide-style sum rules to predict masses for both left-handed and right-handed neutrinos. Combines the Koide constraint on √m with the seesaw integration over a heavy right-handed Majorana sector to over-determine the neutrino spectrum.
