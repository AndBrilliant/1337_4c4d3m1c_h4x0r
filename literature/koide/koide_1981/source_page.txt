Lett. 47 , 1241 – Published 2 November, 1981 DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.1241 Export Citation Show metrics open icon close icon Abstract A composite model for quarks and leptons is proposed. An SU(3)-generation symmetry, which is badly broken not only by the generator F 8 but also by the generator F 3 , is introduced. The model leads to a new formula for the Cabibbo angle θ C represented in terms of lepton masses, which predicts tan θ C = 0.225 .
