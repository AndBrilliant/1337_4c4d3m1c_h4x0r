# Koide 1982 — Original paper introducing the Koide relation

## Reference
Y. Koide, *Fermion-boson two body model of quarks and leptons and Cabibbo mixing*, ***Lett. Nuovo Cim.* **34** (1982) 201**, DOI 10.1007/BF02817096.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 (Origin and exact precision / Original work).

## Beyond-abstract summary
The **original paper** introducing what became known as the Koide relation Q = (mₑ+m_μ+m_τ)/(√mₑ+√m_μ+√m_τ)² = 2/3, embedded as Eq. (17) inside a fermion–boson preon model that also tried to predict CKM angles and quark masses (most of those side-predictions are now ruled out). The paper *predicted* m_τ ≈ 1.777 GeV when the world-average tau mass was 1.7842 GeV — a >2σ deficit at the time. The prediction was vindicated by BES (Bai et al. 1992, PRL 69 3021) and by Belle II 2023.

## Sections / contents
- Fermion–boson composite model (preons) underlying both quarks and leptons.
- Cabibbo-mixing prediction from preon couplings.
- Derivation of Eq. (17): mass sum-rule for charged leptons → Q = 2/3.
- Quark mass and CKM-angle predictions (most subsequently falsified).
- Tau-mass prediction m_τ ≈ 1.777 GeV.

## Status
*Lett. Nuovo Cim.* **34** (1982) 201, peer-reviewed. The foundational paper. The empirical relation Q = 2/3 has held to ~10⁻⁵ over 44 years; the broader preon model is not viable but the lepton-mass relation has survived.
