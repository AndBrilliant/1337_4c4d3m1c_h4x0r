# Koide 1983 PLB — Fermion-boson composite model

## Reference
Y. Koide, *A fermion–boson composite model of quarks and leptons*, ***Phys. Lett. B* **120** (1983) 161**, DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(83)90644-5.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1.

## Beyond-abstract summary
A cleaner restatement of the fermion–boson composite (preon) model first set out in Lett. Nuovo Cim. 34 (1982) 201. Same structural derivation: with mᵢ ∝ gᵢ² and sum constraints Σgᵢ⁽⁸⁾ = 0 and Σ(gᵢ⁽⁸⁾)² = 3(g⁽¹⁾)², the Koide relation Q = 2/3 follows automatically. Quark/CKM side-predictions are now falsified, but the lepton mass relation has survived.

## Sections / contents
- Preon content of the fermion–boson composite model.
- Derivation of mᵢ ∝ gᵢ².
- Sum constraints on the SU(3) flavor charges.
- Resulting Koide relation Q = 2/3.
- Quark and CKM phenomenology (subsequently falsified).
