# Koide 1983 PRD — New view of quark and lepton mass hierarchy

## Reference
Y. Koide, *New view of quark and lepton mass hierarchy*, ***Phys. Rev. D* **28** (1983) 252**; erratum *PRD* **29** (1984) 1544.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Phenomenological reformulation of the Koide relation, with a correction term δ to fit the then-current m_τ value (~1784 MeV, vs. Koide's 1982 prediction of ~1777 MeV). Sets out the "new view of mass hierarchy" framing in which the lepton mass spectrum is understood as a constrained set of three numbers obeying Q = 2/3 plus a 1-parameter free choice. The erratum corrects numerical details.

## Sections / contents
- Restatement of Q = 2/3 mass relation.
- Phenomenological fit with correction δ to match 1983-era tau mass.
- Discussion of the "new view": charged-lepton spectrum as Q-constrained 1-parameter family.
- Implications for quark masses.
- Erratum: PRD 29 (1984) 1544 corrects numerical details.

## Status
*Phys. Rev. D* **28** (1983) 252, peer-reviewed; cited as the major journal-published statement of the relation. The 1982 Lett. Nuovo Cim. paper has priority but PRD 28 is the more accessible reference.
