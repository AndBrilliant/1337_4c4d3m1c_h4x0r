# Koide 1989 — First S₃-symmetric derivation attempt

## Reference
Y. Koide, ***Z. Phys. C* **45** (1989) 39**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §A. Identified as the first S₃-symmetric derivation attempt.

## Beyond-abstract summary
First Koide derivation attempt using a charged-lepton mass matrix with **democratic family mixing**. Sets out the S₃-symmetric mass-matrix framework that became Koide's signature derivation strategy from 1989 through 2018. Achieves Koide at tree level under specific democratic-matrix textures, but radiative corrections are not protected — a problem that eventually motivated the Sumino mechanism (2008).

## Sections / contents
- Charged-lepton mass matrix with S₃ democratic mixing.
- Tree-level derivation of Q = 2/3.
- VEV alignment producing realistic charged-lepton mass hierarchy.
- Radiative-correction issue (foreshadowing the Sumino mechanism).
