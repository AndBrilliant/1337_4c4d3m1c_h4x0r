# Koide 1990 — U(3)-family Higgs scalar potential

## Reference
Y. Koide, ***Mod. Phys. Lett. A* **5** (1990) 2319**.

## Source
Koide literature review research report, §A. The first U(3)-family Higgs nonet derivation attempt.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Constructs a U(3)-family Higgs scalar nonet Φ whose VEV ⟨Φ⟩ ∝ diag(√mₑ, √m_μ, √m_τ) yields the Koide relation as the minimum of a Z₃-symmetric scalar potential. Inaugurates Koide's U(3) family-symmetry program, which Koide himself revisited (hep-ph/9501408, 1995), and which later became the framework Sumino (2008) elevated into the family-gauge-symmetry mechanism.

## Sections / contents
- U(3)-family Higgs scalar nonet Φ structure.
- Z₃-symmetric scalar potential and its minimum.
- VEV ⟨Φ⟩ ∝ diag(√mₑ, √m_μ, √m_τ) producing Q = 2/3.
- Connection to charged-lepton mass hierarchy.
