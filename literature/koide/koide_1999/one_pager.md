## Beyond-abstract summary
Combines the **universal seesaw** mass-matrix structure with an **S₃ family symmetry** to derive the Koide relation in a setting that simultaneously addresses charged leptons and quarks. The S₃ symmetry on the seesaw partners produces the democratic structure naturally, and the seesaw integrates out heavy degrees of freedom producing realistic light-quark hierarchy. Counted as one of Koide's most-cited mechanism papers.

## Sections / contents

- Derivation of Q = 2/3 for charged leptons.
- Cross-sector predictions for quarks and CKM mixing.
