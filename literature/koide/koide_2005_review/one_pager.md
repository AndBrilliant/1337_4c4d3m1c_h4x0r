# Koide 2005 — Challenge to the Mystery of the Charged Lepton Mass Formula

## Reference
Y. Koide, *Challenge to the Mystery of the Charged Lepton Mass Formula*, **arXiv:hep-ph/0506247** → ***Int. J. Mod. Phys. A* **21** (2006) 1**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §A. Identified as Koide's "manifesto / review."

## Beyond-abstract summary
Koide's major review and program statement, written 23 years after the original 1982 paper. Surveys the empirical status of the relation, its derivation attempts, and presents Koide's preferred mechanism: **Z₄ × S₃ symmetry on flavor-triplet scalars**. The paper is the canonical entry point into Koide's own thinking about the relation circa 2005.

## Sections / contents
- Empirical status: 23 years of m_τ refinement converging on Q = 2/3.
- Survey of derivation attempts (S₃, U(3) nonet, democratic seesaw).
- Z₄ × S₃ symmetry on flavor-triplet scalars: Koide's preferred mechanism circa 2005.
- Open problems and future directions.
- Implicit critique of attempts that miss the empirical pole-mass precision.

## Status
*Int. J. Mod. Phys. A* **21** (2006) 1, peer-reviewed. The most-cited Koide review; recommended reading for understanding Koide's mechanism preferences before the Sumino mechanism appeared in 2008.
