# Koide 2007 — S₃ symmetry and neutrino masses & mixings

## Reference
Y. Koide, *S₃ symmetry and neutrino masses and mixings*, ***Eur. Phys. J. C* **50** (2007) 809**. Companion arXiv preprint 0706.2534.

## Source
Koide literature review research report, §A.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Applies the **S₃ family symmetry** framework to the neutrino sector specifically — combining S₃ structure with the universal-seesaw mass-matrix model of Koide 1999 (PRD 60 077301). Provides predictions for neutrino mixings and masses consistent with the tribimaximal pattern, while preserving Koide's charged-lepton relation. Includes citation to Brannen's δ = 2/9.

## Sections / contents
- S₃ symmetric mass matrices for neutrinos.
- Universal-seesaw integrating out heavy partners.
- Tribimaximal MNS predictions.
- Cross-sector consistency with Koide-relation in charged leptons.
