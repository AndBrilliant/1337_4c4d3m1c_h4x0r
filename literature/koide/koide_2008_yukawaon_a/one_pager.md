## Beyond-abstract summary
**Foundational yukawaon paper.** Proposes that the Yukawa coupling matrices Y_f are not fundamental constants but instead are the VEVs of 3×3 scalar fields ⟨Φ_f⟩, called "yukawaons," that transform under family symmetry. The Koide relation is then a constraint on the VEV alignment of the yukawaons. This is the technical machinery Koide pursued for the next decade (2008–2013), with multiple variants.

## Sections / contents
- Yukawaon definition: Y_f = ⟨Φ_f⟩ where Φ_f is a 3×3 scalar field.
- Family-symmetry transformation properties.
- Scalar potential producing the desired VEV alignment.
- Koide relation as a consequence of the symmetric minimum.
- Cross-sector predictions.
