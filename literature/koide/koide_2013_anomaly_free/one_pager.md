# Koide 2013 — Anomaly-free yukawaon variant

## Reference
Y. Koide, **arXiv:1308.2129** (2013).

## Source
Koide-comprehensive research report, §A.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Yukawaon-program variant addressing the **anomaly issue** in the U(3) family gauge symmetry. Sumino's original mechanism (arXiv:0812.2090) and Koide-Yamashita 2012 (arXiv:1203.2028) had to navigate gauge-anomaly constraints; this paper presents an explicitly **anomaly-free** version of the yukawaon framework, paving the way for the 2016 Mod. Phys. Lett. A 32 1750062 paper (arXiv:1608.04514) on Sumino's cancellation in an anomaly-free model.

## Sections / contents
- Anomaly content of the U(3) family gauge symmetry.
- Anomaly-free fermion content.
- Yukawaon scalar potential consistent with anomaly cancellation.
- Phenomenological consequences.
