## Beyond-abstract summary
Develops Sumino's QED-cancellation mechanism in an **explicitly anomaly-free** model. Combines the anomaly-free fermion content of arXiv:1308.2129 with the Sumino radiative-cancellation logic of arXiv:0812.2090 / 0812.2103, producing a complete, gauge-anomaly-consistent realization. One of the late, most-refined Koide-mechanism papers before Koide's 2017 personal-view commentary.
