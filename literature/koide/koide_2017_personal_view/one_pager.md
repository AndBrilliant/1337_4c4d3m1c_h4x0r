# Koide 2017 — Sumino model and my personal view

## Reference
Y. Koide, *Sumino model and my personal view*, **arXiv:1701.01921** (2017).

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §A. Identified explicitly as "commentary."

## Beyond-abstract summary
Koide's reflection on the Koide relation 35 years after the original 1982 paper. Explicitly acknowledges Q = 2/3 as a **pole-mass coincidence whose excellent agreement may itself be accidental** even if a U(3) model gives K = 2/3 at high scale. Marks an unusual degree of self-doubt from the architect of the program; recommended reading for anyone evaluating whether the Sumino mechanism really resolves the puzzle.

## Sections / contents
- Personal recollection of the 1982 original paper and the early years.
- Honest assessment of the Sumino mechanism's strengths and weaknesses.
- Acknowledgement that Q = 2/3 *might* be a pole-mass-specific coincidence.
- Discussion of whether high-scale K = 2/3 is physically motivated.
- Open problems for the next generation of work.
