## Beyond-abstract summary
Koide's late-period synthesis review of the Koide-relation literature, with emphasis on what the empirical persistence of Q = 2/3 to 10⁻⁵ precision tells us about underlying physics. Reviews the Sumino mechanism, the yukawaon program, the discrete-flavor approaches, and the geometric/circulant readings, and offers Koide's mature synthesis circa 2018.
