## Beyond-abstract summary
Companion to hep-ph/9505201 / Z. Phys. C 71 459. Develops the **democratic-seesaw mass-matrix** structure with a specific b_u = −1/3 corner element that reproduces realistic up-type quark masses while preserving the Koide-style structure for the charged leptons. The "democratic seesaw" labeling becomes Koide's preferred terminology for cross-sector mass-matrix attempts through the late 1990s.
