# Koide & Fusaoka 1996 — Top-quark seesaw

## Reference
Y. Koide and H. Fusaoka, **arXiv:hep-ph/9505201** → ***Z. Phys. C* **71** (1996) 459**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §1 / §A. Cited as the early seesaw extension to quarks.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Extends the Koide framework to the quark sector via a **seesaw structure** in the top-quark mass. Provides the technical setup that Koide & Fusaoka 1996 (hep-ph/9508369) and Koide 1996 (hep-ph/9602303) refined into the "democratic seesaw" with b_u = -1/3 corner.

## Sections / contents
- Seesaw mass-matrix construction for the up-type quarks.
- Top-quark mass treated as the heavy seesaw partner.
- Koide-style charged-lepton constraint preserved.
- Initial cross-sector predictions.

## Status
*Z. Phys. C* **71** (1996) 459, peer-reviewed. First Koide-quark seesaw attempt; superseded by the democratic seesaw of Koide 1996 (hep-ph/9602303).
