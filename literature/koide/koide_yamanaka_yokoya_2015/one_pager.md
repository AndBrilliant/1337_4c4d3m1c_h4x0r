# Koide, Yamanaka & Yokoya 2015 — LHC production of family gauge bosons

## Reference
Y. Koide, M. Yamanaka, and H. Yokoya, **arXiv:1507.04987** (2015).

## Source
Koide-comprehensive research report, §A.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Computes the **LHC production cross-section for family gauge bosons** in the Sumino / Koide-Yamashita framework. Provides the direct-search strategy for the TeV-scale FGBs that Sumino's mechanism predicts. Explicit signatures: lepton-flavor-violating dijet / dilepton resonances at the LHC.

## Sections / contents
- LHC production cross-sections for FGBs.
- Decay channels: ll, lν, jj signatures.
- Lepton-flavor-violating final states (e.g. eμ, eτ, μτ).
- Constraints from existing LHC dijet/dilepton searches.
- Search strategy for HL-LHC.
