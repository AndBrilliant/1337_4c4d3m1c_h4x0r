# Li & Ma 2005 — Quark-lepton complementarity

## Reference
N. Li and B.-Q. Ma, **arXiv:hep-ph/0505028** → ***Phys. Lett. B* **609** (2005) 309**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §5 / §12. Identified as the source of the **k_l + k_d ≈ k_ν + k_u ≈ 2** complementarity relation.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Pre-cursor to Li-Ma 2006 PRD. Establishes the **quark-lepton complementarity** k_l + k_d ≈ k_ν + k_u ≈ 2, where k_f is the Koide-style ratio for fermion sector f. The complementarity ties the Koide ratios across all four sectors and provides the cross-sector constraint that Cao 2012 (PRD 85 113003) later used to predict absolute neutrino masses.

## Sections / contents
- Definition of Koide-style ratios k_f for charged leptons, neutrinos, up- and down-quarks.
- Empirical complementarity k_l + k_d ≈ k_ν + k_u ≈ 2.
- Cross-sector implications.
- Initial neutrino-mass estimate.

## Status
*Phys. Lett. B* **609** (2005) 309, peer-reviewed. Foundational for cross-sector / "universal" Koide proposals (Kartavtsev 2011, Cao 2012, Gao-Li 2016).
