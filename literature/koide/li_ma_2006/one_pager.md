# Li & Ma 2006 — Energy scale independence of Koide's relation

## Reference
N. Li and B.-Q. Ma, *Energy scale independence of Koide's relation for quark and lepton masses*, **arXiv:hep-ph/0601031** → ***Phys. Rev. D* **73** (2006) 013009**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §5 / §G.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Argues that Koide's relation is **approximately RG-stable** under running between low and high scales, in some tension with Xing-Zhang 2006 (PLB 635 107) which finds K^run(M_Z) deviates from 2/3 by 0.2%. The Koide-comprehensive Caveats section explicitly notes: "Li & Ma's 2006 RG-stability claim sits in tension with Xing-Zhang's K^run(M_Z) = (2/3)(1.00189); a careful reader should look at both."

## Sections / contents
- RG analysis of Koide's relation across scales.
- Quark-lepton complementarity claims.
- Argument for energy-scale independence.
- Implicit tension with the Xing-Zhang 0.2% deviation finding.

## Status
