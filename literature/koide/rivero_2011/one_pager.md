## Beyond-abstract summary
Rivero's most-cited individual contribution to Koide phenomenology. Observes that with √m_s taken with **negative sign**, (s, c, b) form a Koide triplet quasi-orthogonal to (e, μ, τ). Proposes the **"Koide waterfall"**: chaining s-c-b → c-b-t → ... gives m_t ≈ 173.26 GeV. The negative-sign requirement parallels Brannen 2006's neutrino sign flip on √m_ν1, and provides one of the empirical hooks for the Z₂ part of any C₃ × Z₂ symmetry hypothesis (the Z₂ parity of √m).
