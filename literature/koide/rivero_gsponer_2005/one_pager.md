# Rivero & Gsponer 2005 — The strange formula of Dr. Koide

## Reference
A. Rivero and A. Gsponer, *The strange formula of Dr. Koide*, **arXiv:hep-ph/0505220** (May 2005). Never journal-published.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §2 / §B. Identified as the **canonical English-language entry point** to the Koide-relation literature.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The standard historical and bibliographical review of the Koide relation. Provides the canonical English-language entry point, surveys derivation attempts (Foot, Adler, Kiselev, Krolikowski, Koide), introduces the **"gim" notation J² = m** to remove square roots from the Koide combination, and observes Koide-like fits for down-quarks (b, s, d) using Barut masses (a precursor to Rivero's later strange-charm-bottom proposal in arXiv:1111.7232). Often (e.g. on Wikipedia) credited with first noting the c-b-t coincidence, though the Koide literature review research report's caveats correct this — Rodejohann-Zhang v1 / Cao 2012 hold priority.

## Sections / contents
- History of the Koide relation 1982 → 2005.

- "gim" notation J² = m.
- Koide-like fits for down-quarks (b, s, d) using Barut masses.
- Discussion of whether the relation is fundamental or coincidence.
