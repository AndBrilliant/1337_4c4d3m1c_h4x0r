## Beyond-abstract summary
Major Koide-extension paper. Extends the charged-lepton Koide relation to neutrinos. **Critical priority note:** v1 of the arXiv preprint (January 2011) contained an explicit observation that Q ≈ 2/3 holds for both light (u,d,s) and heavy (c,b,t) quark triplets — but this heavy-triplet observation was **removed from the published PLB version**. The first peer-reviewed mention of the c-b-t Koide tuple therefore belongs to Cao 2012 (PRD 85 113003). Both Koide literature review research reports recommend a **dual citation Rodejohann-Zhang v1 / Cao 2012** to avoid priority disputes.
