## Beyond-abstract summary
**The most influential Koide-mechanism paper.** Verbatim from the abstract: "We propose a mechanism for cancelling the QED correction to Koide's formula. This is discussed in an effective theory with U(3) family gauge symmetry and a scenario in which this symmetry is unified with SU(2)_L symmetry at 10²–10³ TeV scale." The mechanism: gauge a U(3) family symmetry; arrange (M_ij)² ∝ (m_i + m_j) for family gauge bosons; the FGB radiative correction log(M_ii/μ)² cancels the QED log(m_i/μ)² in the pole-mass running, so Koide can be valid simultaneously at high scale **and** for pole masses.
