# Xing 2010 — Broken-S₃ flavor-symmetry pattern

## Reference

## Beyond-abstract summary
Develops a **broken-S₃ flavor symmetry** pattern for fermion mass matrices in the form M = ξI + ζD, where I is the identity and D the democratic matrix. Provides a structural ansatz that produces Koide-type sum rules and CKM-mixing predictions in a controlled way.

## Sections / contents
