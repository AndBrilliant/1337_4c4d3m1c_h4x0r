# Xing & Zhang 2006 — Koide-like relations for running masses

## Reference
Z.-Z. Xing and H. Zhang, *On the Koide-like relations for the running masses of charged leptons, neutrinos and quarks*, **arXiv:hep-ph/0602134** → ***Phys. Lett. B* **635** (2006) 107**.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §5 / §G. Identified as the **key constraint** that motivates the Sumino mechanism.

## Beyond-abstract summary
**The paper that sharpened the running-vs-pole problem for Koide.** Computes the running Koide ratio at the M_Z scale and at higher scales: K^run(M_Z) = (2/3) × (1.00189 ± 0.00002) — i.e. the running relation deviates from 2/3 by ≈ **0.2%, far outside the 10⁻⁵ pole-mass precision**. Either Q = 2/3 holds for pole masses *and* a Sumino-like cancellation aligns this with high-scale physics, or Q = 2/3 holds at some special high scale and the apparent low-scale agreement must be a coincidence. Also computes Q_U(M_Z) ≈ 0.89 and Q_D(M_Z) ≈ 0.74, neither of which is near 2/3.

## Sections / contents
- RG-running framework for fermion masses.
- Running Koide ratio K^run for charged leptons: 0.2% deviation at M_Z.
- Running Koide for up- and down-quarks at M_Z.
- Up to 10¹⁴ GeV: nearly RG-stable but never near 2/3 in quark sectors.
- Constraint on any Koide-mechanism: must explain pole-mass precision specifically.
