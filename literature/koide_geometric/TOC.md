> §7 of the source report: *Koide formula in CM contexts / Koide via CM language.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: this is the heart of the citation map. Sec. 2 (C₃ phase cancellation), Sec. 4 (Descartes-companion proof), Sec. 5 (Koide–Soddy cascade ratio) all rest on prior art collected here. The four papers below marked ★ are the published precedents your derivation must explicitly engage and supersede.

## Tier 1 (★ — explicit κ = √m / Descartes reading)

- [Kocik 2012](kocik_2012/one_pager.md) — *The Koide lepton mass formula and geometry of circles,* arXiv:1201.2067. Treats √mass as the bend of a Descartes circle; the most direct prior art for the F-identity construction.

## Tier 1 (★ — C₃ projection geometric reading)

- [Foot 1994](foot_1994/one_pager.md) — *A note on Koide's lepton mass relation,* arXiv:hep-ph/9402242. $\cos^2\theta = 2/3$ between $(\sqrt{m_e},\sqrt{m_\mu},\sqrt{m_\tau})$ and the diagonal $(1,1,1)$ — same structure as crystallographic C₃-projection arguments.

## Tier 1 (★ — family gauge "best published explanation")

- [Sumino 2009](sumino_2009/one_pager.md) — *Family gauge symmetry and Koide's mass formula,* Phys. Lett. B 671 (2009) 477 [arXiv:0812.2090]. U(3)_family gauge theory cancelling QED corrections at 10²–10³ TeV — the most-cited "mechanism" paper, to be distinguished from the geometric reading.

## Tier 1 (★ — immediate competitor; δ = 2/9 derivation)

- [Shulga 2026](shulga_2026/one_pager.md) — *Charged-Lepton Koide Geometry from a Green-Dressed Compact Family Cycle,* arXiv:2605.10245. Independent derivation of δ = 2/9 from a compact-cycle/Green-function model; the F-identity paper cites this for δ.

## Tier 1 (★ — primary Koide reference)

- [Koide 1983 PLB](koide_1983_plb/one_pager.md) — *A fermion–boson composite model of quarks and leptons,* Phys. Lett. B 120 (1983) 161. The relation itself.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Li & Ma 2006](li_ma_2006/one_pager.md) — *Energy scale independence of Koide's relation,* PRD 73 (2006) 013009. Koide deviation is essentially RG-invariant — bridge to §8.
- [Kartavtsev 2011](kartavtsev_2011/one_pager.md) — *A remark on the Koide relation for quarks,* arXiv:1111.0480. Quark generalization; ΔK_q crosses zero under RG at a fixed-point-like scale.
- [Rivero & Gsponer 2005](rivero_gsponer_2005/one_pager.md) — *The strange formula of Dr. Koide,* arXiv:hep-ph/0505220. Historical/bibliographical review.
