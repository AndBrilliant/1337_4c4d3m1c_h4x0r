# Kartavtsev 2011 — A remark on the Koide relation for quarks

## Reference
A. Kartavtsev, *A remark on the Koide relation for quarks*, **arXiv:1111.0480** (2 Nov 2011); MPI Kernphysik, Heidelberg.

## Source
§7 (Koide formula in CM contexts), tier ✦ — quark-sector Koide; RG-crossing observation.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Two-page note proposing a six-quark generalization $K_q = \sum m_q / (\sum\sqrt{m_q})^2$ summing over up- and down-type quarks of all three generations. Using PDG-2010 values, the deviation from the Koide limit $K = 2/3$ is $-5\cdot 10^{-2} < \Delta K_q < -4\cdot 10^{-2}$ at pole masses; using $m_q(M_Z)$ running masses, $2\cdot 10^{-2} < \Delta K_q < 5\cdot 10^{-2}$. **Crucially: $\Delta K_q$ crosses zero between 2 GeV and $M_Z$** — i.e. there exists an RG scale at which $K_q = 2/3$ for the full six-quark vector. In a six-dimensional vector space, this corresponds to the angle between $(\sqrt{m_d}, \sqrt{m_u}, \sqrt{m_s}, \ldots)$ and the symmetric vector $(1,1,1,\ldots)$ being $\approx \pi/3$. The relation extends the geometric interpretation of Foot 1994 to quarks.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
The "fixed-point-like" RG-scale observation is a useful supporting citation if a referee asks whether the F-identity construction's quark predictions (which use a single scale $\mu_\star$) are sensitive to RG scale choice. Kartavtsev's observation shows the quark Koide deviation passes through zero under RG flow, supporting the idea that there is a privileged scale where geometric relations are most cleanly realized.
