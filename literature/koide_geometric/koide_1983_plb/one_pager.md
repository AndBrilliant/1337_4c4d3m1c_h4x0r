## Beyond-abstract summary
A cleaner restatement of the fermion–boson composite (preon) model first set out in Koide 1982. Quarks and leptons are taken as bound states of a heavy fermion (preon) and a colored scalar, with masses $m_i \propto g_i^2$ where $g_i$ are the coupling strengths to a confining $SU(3)$-like flavor force. The sum constraints $\sum_i g_i^{(8)} = 0$ (traceless octet component) and $\sum_i (g_i^{(8)})^2 = 3(g^{(1)})^2$ (normalization of octet vs. singlet) then yield $Q \equiv \sum m_i / (\sum\sqrt{m_i})^2 = 2/3$ automatically. The preon model also generates Cabibbo-mixing and quark mass predictions, most of which were subsequently falsified; the charged-lepton mass relation has survived.

## Use in the F-identity manuscript
Cited as `Koide1983` (paired with `Koide1982`) in Sec. 1 of the manuscript as the origin reference for the Koide relation $Q = 2/3$. The F-identity construction shifts the *derivation* from preon-mass-squared sum rules to a $C_3 \times Z_2$ geometric identity, so the F-identity paper retains Koide 1983's *result* while replacing its *mechanism*. The Koide 1983 preon model and the F-identity paper are *not* in direct competition; the F-identity paper is silent on what physically realizes $C_3 \times Z_2$ at high scale (it offers geometry, not dynamics).
