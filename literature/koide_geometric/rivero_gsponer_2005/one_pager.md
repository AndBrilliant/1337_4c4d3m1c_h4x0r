## Beyond-abstract summary
A 10-page review treating Koide's formula historically and bibliographically, plus three speculative extension directions. Sections: (1) a subjective history of the formula's reception, (2) extension to quarks and neutrinos via the "gim" notation $\gimel \equiv \sqrt{m}$, (3) alternate origins (asymmetric Weyl spinors with degenerate-$M$ + non-degenerate-$M'$; non-commutative geometry à la Connes–Lott; switching-off-couplings thought experiment in which the muon/tau ratio approaches $((1+\sqrt 3)/(1-\sqrt 3))^2 \approx 14$, exactly the Koide limit with $m_e = 0$). The Cabibbo-angle observation (Brannen): the matrix $\sqrt{2}\,I_3 + M(\theta)$ with $M_{ij} = e^{i\theta_{ij}}$ (cyclic) has eigenvalues proportional to $\sqrt{m_\ell}$ when $\theta$ equals the Cabibbo angle. This last connection is what motivates the F-identity manuscript's downstream use of Brannen's parametrization.
