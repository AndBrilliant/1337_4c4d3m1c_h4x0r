# Sumino 2009 — Family gauge symmetry and Koide's mass formula

## Reference
Y. Sumino, *Family Gauge Symmetry and Koide's Mass Formula*, **Phys. Lett. B 671 (2009) 477–480**; **arXiv:0812.2090v2** (8 Jan 2009); TU-832, Department of Physics, Tohoku University.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The most influential mechanism paper for Koide's relation. The empirical Koide relation $Q=2/3$ holds for *pole* masses to one part in $10^5$; the 1-loop QED correction shifts the running-vs-pole conversion by $\approx 0.1\%$, $\sim 120\times$ the experimental error. Hence any high-scale mechanism that produces Koide must come with a cancellation against the QED correction. Sumino constructs such a mechanism: an effective theory with $U(3)$ family (horizontal) gauge symmetry, with $\psi_L \in (3,1)$ and $e_R \in (\bar 3, -1)$, so the dimension-4 operator $\bar\psi_L \varphi e_R$ is forbidden by symmetry. The charged-lepton mass matrix is induced by $\mathcal{O} = (\kappa/\Lambda^2)\bar\psi_L \Phi\Phi^T \varphi e_R$, with the family-gauge boson loop generating $\delta m_i^{\rm pole} = -(3\alpha_F/8\pi)[\log(\mu^2/v_i^2) + c]\,m_i$. The form of the correction *mimics* the QED correction, and the cancellation condition is $\alpha = \alpha_F/4$. Since $\frac{1}{4}\alpha_W(\mu) \approx \alpha(m_\tau)$ at $\mu \sim 10^2$–$10^3$ TeV, an electroweak–family unification at that scale naturally realizes the relation. Predicts lepton-flavor-violating processes (e.g. $K_L \to e\mu$) at 100–1000 TeV scale.

## Sections / contents
- §I (no labeled): empirical statement of Koide; the QED radiative-correction problem.
- §II: $U(3)$ family-gauge representations; $(3,1) \oplus (\bar 3,-1)$ choice; tree-level mass matrix from $\Phi\Phi$.
- §III: 1-loop computation in Landau gauge; $\delta m_i \propto m_i \log v_i^2$ structure forced by $U(1)^3$ residual symmetry; cancellation condition $\alpha = \alpha_F/4$.
- §IV: scenario for electroweak–family unification at $10^2$–$10^3$ TeV; LFV predictions including $K_L \to e\mu$.

## Use in the F-identity manuscript
Cited as `Sumino2009` in the Discussion as "the radiative protection mechanism" — credited in acknowledgments. Sumino represents the alternative explanation paradigm: a *gauge mechanism* that cancels QED corrections. The F-identity paper takes a different route — a *geometric* derivation via $C_3 \times Z_2$ identities — and the Discussion makes this distinction explicit. Sumino's empirical observation that Koide holds for pole masses (not running masses) is consistent with the F-identity paper's use of pole-mass inputs $\mu_\star = m_e + m_\mu + m_\tau = 1883.1$ MeV.
