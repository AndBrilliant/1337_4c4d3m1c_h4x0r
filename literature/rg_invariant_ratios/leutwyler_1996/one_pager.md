# Leutwyler 1996 — The ratios of the light quark masses

## Reference
H. Leutwyler, *The ratios of the light quark masses*, **Phys. Lett. B 378 (1996) 313–318**; **arXiv:hep-ph/9602366** (22 Feb 1996); CERN-TH/96-44; Institut für theoretische Physik, Universität Bern and CERN.

## Source
- Research report §8 (RG-invariant mass ratios), tier ★ — flagged as "the canonical citation" for "$m_s/m_d$ and $m_u/m_d$ are RG-invariant pure numbers."

## Beyond-abstract summary
A 12-page synthesis of the chiral-perturbation-theory and lattice constraints on the light-quark mass ratios. Three foundational facts: (i) $\bar u u$, $\bar d d$, $\bar s s$ transform under $(3,3^*) \oplus (3^*,3)$ of SU(3)$_L\times$SU(3)$_R$ in mass-independent renormalization schemes, so the running quark masses differ between schemes only by a *flavor-independent* factor; the ratios $m_u/m_d$ and $m_s/m_d$ are therefore "convention-independent pure numbers." (ii) Leading-order chiral PT plus electromagnetic corrections gives $m_u/m_d \approx 0.55$, $m_s/m_d \approx 20.1$. (iii) Combining the elliptic constraint from chiral PT, the large-$N_c$ bound $\Delta_M > 0$, and the $\psi' \to \psi\pi^0 / \psi\eta$ ratio gives the final values $m_u/m_d = 0.553 \pm 0.043$, $m_s/m_d = 18.9 \pm 0.8$, $m_s/m_u = 34.4 \pm 3.7$. Running masses at $\mu = 1$ GeV in $\overline{\rm MS}$: $m_u = 5.1 \pm 0.9$, $m_d = 9.3 \pm 1.4$, $m_s = 175 \pm 25$ MeV.

## Sections / contents
- 1. Introduction: chiral-PT update of earlier work.
- 2. RG-invariance of ratios; flavor-independent anomalous dimension $\gamma_m$.
- 3. Gell-Mann–Oakes–Renner leading-order mass formulae; Dashen theorem.
- 4-5. NLO corrections; the elliptic constraint $Q^2 = (m_s^2 - \hat m^2)/(m_d^2 - m_u^2)$.
- 6. Dashen-theorem corrections; lattice EM splittings.
- 7. $\eta \to 3\pi$ determination of $Q$.
- 8-9. Kaplan–Manohar reparametrization invariance; large-$N_c$ resolution.
- 10. $\psi' \to \psi\pi^0/\psi\eta$ constraint.
- 11. Final values and running-mass output.

## Use in the F-identity manuscript
Cited (indirectly via FLAG2024 in the manuscript) as the foundational reference for treating $m_s/m_d$ as a scale-independent pure number. The F-identity construction *predicts* $m_s/m_d = R_K = 1/\alpha_K^2 = 19.798$, consistent with Leutwyler's 18.9 ± 0.8 at $1.0\sigma$. If a referee challenges the scale-invariance assumption used to compare a single-scale theoretical prediction to FLAG/PDG ratios, Leutwyler 1996 (plus Gasser–Leutwyler 1982 and Li–Ma 2006 for Koide-RG) is the citation chain.
