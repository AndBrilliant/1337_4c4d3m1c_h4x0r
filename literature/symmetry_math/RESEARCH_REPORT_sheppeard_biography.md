# Marni Dee Sheppeard (1967–2020/2021): Work on the Koide Relation, Circulant Mass Matrices, and Categorical Particle Physics

> **Source:** Claude web research mode output, retrieved 2026-05-08. Filed under `symmetry_math/` because the report's central technical content is Sheppeard's categorical / Z₃-Fourier / circulant-matrix reformulation of Koide-type mass relations.

## TL;DR
- Marni Dee Sheppeard was a New Zealand–based theoretical physicist (b. Sydney, 1967; d. November–December 2020 in the Southern Alps, remains found late March 2021) whose central scientific contribution was a categorical/algebraic reformulation of Koide-type lepton and quark mass relations using circulant matrices and the discrete Fourier transform over Z₃, developed in close public collaboration with Carl Brannen; the user's prompt placing her death "around 2014" is incorrect — she remained active on viXra and Twitter until November 2020.
- Her published record splits cleanly: a small early arXiv presence (notably the 2003 Crane–Sheppeard paper math/0306440 and the withdrawn gr-qc/0404121 from 2004), her 2007 University of Canterbury PhD thesis "Gluon Phenomenology and a Linear Topos," and approximately 28 viXra preprints from 2007–2020 (a figure given by David Chester in the 2021 Quantum Gravity Research YouTube tribute "The Legacy of Dr. Marni Sheppeard"); she lost arXiv endorsement after her PhD and was forced to use viXra thereafter.
- Her ideas (categorified twistor amplitudes, Bilson-Thompson braids decorated with Brannen-Koide circulant phase data, mirror-neutrino cosmology) anticipated several themes that later became fashionable (Arkani-Hamed's amplituhedron program, motivic structure of scattering amplitudes), but she received almost no academic recognition during her lifetime; she lived in poverty and intermittent homelessness, and died alone while hiking near Arthur's Pass.

## Key Findings

**1. Identity and timeline.** Marni Dee Sheppeard was born in Sydney in 1967 and was a dual Australian–New Zealand citizen who blogged under the pseudonym "Kea." She held research positions at the Perimeter Institute (Waterloo) and the University of Oxford after her PhD, then returned to New Zealand. She was last heard from on Twitter at 5:20 a.m. on 19 November 2020 ("off to the mountains"), reported missing in January 2021, and human remains believed to be hers were located near Otira, Arthur's Pass National Park, in late March 2021. The user's premise that she died "around 2014" appears to conflate her death with the 2013 wave of online concern about her destitution; she was alive and publishing through 2020.

**2. arXiv papers.** The arXiv record under "M.D. Sheppeard" is small:
- **L. Crane and M.D. Sheppeard, "2-categorical Poincaré Representations and State Sum Applications," arXiv:math/0306440 (June 30, 2003)** — co-authored with Louis Crane (Kansas State / Perimeter), this is her main refereed-style arXiv contribution. It builds Lie 2-group representation theory aimed at a Poincaré 2-category state-sum model of 4D quantum gravity. Sheppeard is listed with affiliation "Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Canterbury, Christchurch, New Zealand."
- **arXiv:gr-qc/0404121 (April 2004)** — a single-author follow-up paper that was withdrawn shortly after submission. Mitchell Porter notes it "shows that she was talking about Goncharov years before the twistor world discovered his relevance," suggesting it touched motivic/cohomological themes; the withdrawal is consistent with her later loss of arXiv endorsement.

After 2004 she lost the ability to post to arXiv. Her InspireHEP author profile is at https://inspirehep.net/authors/1019492.

**3. PhD thesis (Canterbury, 2007).** Title: **"Gluon Phenomenology and a Linear Topos,"** Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Canterbury, Christchurch (2007). UC Research Repository: https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/handle/10092/1436. Self-hosted PDF: arcadianfunctor.files.wordpress.com/2007/11/thesismds.pdf. The thesis applies category theory and topos theory to QFT observables, develops an "abstract categorical Machian principle of duality for a ribbon graph calculus" intended to incorporate Carl Brannen's Koide-type mass calculations and Sundance Bilson-Thompson's preon braid scheme, derives Veneziano-type n-point functions using cohomological techniques inspired by twistor theory and MHV amplitudes, and gives "axioms for a quantum topos." It is unusual in combining a high-formal-machinery thesis (categorical/topos) with a phenomenology chapter on Koide circulants — the signature of her later viXra program.

**4. viXra papers.** From 2007 onward she posted approximately 28 papers on viXra (https://vixra.org/author/m_d_sheppeard). Confirmed individual papers include:
- **viXra:0907.0011** — paper on the mixing matrix (linked as "mixing matrix" from her blog sidebar, July 2009).
- **viXra:1004.0083** — extended Bilson-Thompson mapping (April 2010).
- **viXra:1010.0029** — October 2010 synthesis paper containing the page-17 derivation of the Brannen-Koide angle of 4/27 radians for down quarks via a triality transformation acting on the 2/9-radian charged-lepton angle.
- **viXra:1107.0002** — major July 2011 motivic/categorical synthesis paper outlining her program of mapping a Jordan-algebra category to a category of braids via "motives," producing Brannen-style Koide mass matrices.
- **viXra:1102.0010** — G. Dungworth and M.D. Sheppeard (Feb 2011), the first joint Dungworth–Sheppeard paper on neutrino/CMB mass relations.
- **viXra:1406.0125** — "The Associahedra and Permutohedra Yet Again" (June 2014).
- **viXra:1411.0587** — "A Simple Lecture on MUBs" (mutually unbiased bases, Nov 2014).
- **viXra:1507.0169** — "Lectures on Affine, Hyperbolic and Quantum Algebras" (July 2015).
- **viXra:1709.0035** — first phenomenology paper after a long hiatus (Sep 2017).
- **viXra:1711.0119** — Dungworth and Sheppeard, "Non Local Mirror Neutrinos with R=ct" (Nov 2017), which notes the coincidence between the CMB temperature and the central mirror-neutrino mass under the Koide relation, namely 0.00117 eV.
- **viXra:1711.0336** — **"Lepton mass phases and the CKM matrix"** (Nov 2017, v3): the most cited late paper. Abstract: "The Brannen neutrino mass triplet extends Koide's rule for the charged leptons, which was used to correctly predict the τ mass. Assuming that Koide's rule is exact, we consider the fundamental 2/9 lepton phase, noting connections to the CKM matrix and arithmetic information. An estimate for the fine structure constant α is included."

A 2018 paper of hers contains a derivation of a 1 eV neutrino mass scale by multiplying her "neutrino CMB correspondence" mass scale (~0.00117 eV) by an appropriate factor; this scale is independently relevant for LSND/MiniBooNE anomalies and superfluid-MOND dark matter (Khoury–Berezhiani).

**5. Carl Brannen collaboration.** This is the heart of her phenomenological program. The Brannen–Sheppeard collaboration began on the Physics Forums online discussion (~2005–2006) after Carl Brannen rewrote Koide's relation as an eigenvalue equation for a 3×3 circulant matrix. Sheppeard recognized that Brannen's reformulation amounts to a discrete Fourier transform over Z₃ acting on the square roots of charged-lepton masses, and proposed extending this with a non-abelian/non-commutative DFT for the quark sector. Their joint preprint, **C.A. Brannen and M.D. Sheppeard, "The Discrete Fourier Transform and the Particle Mixing Matrices,"** was hosted at brannenworks.com/Gravity/PRD_NewParamRefs.pdf (the URL stub suggests an unsuccessful PRD submission). It argues that the CKM and MNS matrices are best decomposed as sums of 1-circulant and 2-circulant 3×3 matrices, with the tribimaximal MNS form admitting an especially clean (1/3, 2/3) decomposition. Brannen has continued to credit her — in 2021 he wrote on Physics Forums that "Marni Sheppeard recognized the way I redid the Koide formula as a Discrete Fourier Transform and supposed that what we needed was a Discrete Fourier Transform for a non-Abelian (well she said non commutative) symmetry." Brannen's "Koide mass equations for hadrons" paper (brannenworks.com/koidehadrons.pdf) credits Sheppeard for the DFT observation and refers to a forthcoming joint paper on the CKM and MNS matrices as 1-circulant + 2-circulant sums.

**6. Discrete symmetries / Z₃ / Fourier picture.** This is the most technically distinctive thread in her work. The 3×3 circulant matrix C(A,B,C) has eigenvalues A+B+C and √(A²+B²+C²−AB−BC−CA), which Brannen showed reproduces Koide's 2/3 ratio when applied to a particular charged-lepton mass triplet; the diagonalising matrix is precisely the 3-point DFT (Fourier transform over Z₃), with the cube root of unity ω = exp(2πi/3) playing the central role. Sheppeard reinterpreted this as a manifestation of an underlying C₃/Z₃ generation symmetry sitting inside a categorical "qutrit" structure, and pushed it further:
- The **2/9 radian lepton phase** is identified as a fundamental phase invariant.
- The **MNS (PMNS) tribimaximal matrix** decomposes naturally as the product F₃F₂ of two quantum Fourier operators (a 3-point and a 2-point DFT), corresponding to a qubit ⊗ qutrit (p=2 ⊗ p=3) tensor product.
- The CKM matrix is treated as a perturbation of a circulant + 2-circulant decomposition involving 12th roots of unity.
- The **down-quark Brannen-Koide angle of 4/27 radians** is derived from the 2/9 charged-lepton angle by a **triality** transformation acting on the relevant exceptional Jordan algebra J₃(O), with mirror-neutrino factors of 2/9 cancelling between left and right components to leave a residual π/6 phase (R³ = iI in her Cayley B₃ representation).
- The whole structure is recast in the language of **mutually unbiased bases (MUBs)** for Hilbert spaces of dimension p^n, providing a quantum-information rationale for why exactly these "magic" matrices appear.

**7. Category theory / monoidal / operads / higher categories.** Her categorical work runs in parallel:
- **Tricategories / 2-groups**: the Crane–Sheppeard 2003 paper develops Lie 2-group representation theory for a 4D Poincaré state-sum model.
- **Operads and associahedra**: she argued in numerous blog posts and in her June 2014 viXra paper "The Associahedra and Permutohedra Yet Again" that Stasheff's associahedra (the operad of binary trees / planar tree composition) underlie tree-level scattering and connect to the permutohedra via cube tilings. In a November 2016 retrospective, blogger Tom Copeland writes that "It was Marni Sheppeard through her paper 'Constructive Motives and Scattering' who first gave me a docent's tour of this tapestry" — the tapestry interweaving Grassmannians, associahedra, cluster algebras, MZVs, generalised permutohedra, and scattering amplitudes — well before Arkani-Hamed's amplituhedron paper appeared.
- **Motives**: the unifying theme of her late work was that Grothendieck's motives (a universal cohomology theory) provide a canonical mapping from a category of measurement algebras (Schwinger / exceptional Jordan) to a category of geometric objects (braids), inducing the Brannen-Koide mass matrices. This program is laid out most fully in viXra:1107.0002.
- **Topos**: her PhD thesis title "Gluon Phenomenology and a Linear **Topos**" indicates the use of topos theory, in the line of Chris Isham's earlier work, as a foundational language for QFT observables.

**8. Blogs.** She maintained three sequential blogs:
- **Arcadian Functor** (kea-monad.blogspot.com), May 2006 – April 2010. The "M Theory Lessons" series ran from 2007 through Lesson 312 in April 2010 and is the densest accessible record of her thinking. Lessons 309–312 (April 2010) explicitly assign 12th-root-of-unity matrices to ribbon-twist diagrams, define a "twisted Fourier transform" F C_a F† = D C_b that maps MUB-type operators C_a to "particle circulants" C_b, and exhibit a quark-lepton complementarity in which a product of anti-up and anti-down quark matrices yields a positron in dual braid space. The blog has a rich blogroll linking Carl Brannen, Louise Riofrio, Michael Rios, Matti Pitkänen, John Baez, and Ross Street.
- **Arcadian Pseudofunctor** (pseudomonad.blogspot.com), 2010–2013, where the "Theory Update" series (e.g. Theory Update 105, 125) continued the same lines.
- **Arcadian Omegafunctor** / later Typepad blog "Arcadian Gravity" (arcadiangravity.typepad.com), preserved at the Internet Archive (e.g. https://web.archive.org/web/20210801160842/https://arcadiangravity.typepad.com/).
A scribd-archived "Arcadian Functor 09" (https://www.scribd.com/document/19799877/ArcadianFunctor09) collects the 2009 posts as a single PDF.

**10. Biographical context.** She first became publicly known in New Zealand in December 2003 when she and Sonja Rendell survived eight days lost in the Southern Alps, sheltered for seven nights in a coffin-sized rock cavity — the episode was retold in the Discovery Channel series *I Shouldn't Be Alive*. After the PhD she held short stints at Perimeter Institute (Waterloo) and Oxford. Returning to New Zealand, she worked variously as a waitress, dishwasher, and lattice-QCD technician, repeatedly waited on tables to fund her own research, attributed much of her career failure to academic sexism (which she discussed openly on her blogs), and by 2019 was homeless on the streets of Auckland, briefly housed in a City Mission shelter. She gave a talk at the **32nd International Colloquium on Group Theoretical Methods in Physics ("Group32"), held at Czech Technical University in Prague, 9–13 July 2018** (proceedings J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. vol. 1194, 2019). Her last sustained period of academic engagement was a 2015 visit to John Baez in Auckland and a 2018 trip to the USA. She died alone in the mountains she loved.

## Details

### How the Z₃/circulant/Koide thread actually works (technical synopsis)
Koide's empirical relation for the charged leptons,

(√mₑ + √m_μ + √m_τ)² / (mₑ + m_μ + m_τ) = 3/2,

is equivalent to the statement that the vector (√mₑ, √m_μ, √m_τ) makes an angle of arccos(1/√(3/2)) with the equal-component vector (1,1,1) — equivalently, 2/9 radians is one of the Brannen-style "phase" parameters in a particular trigonometric reparametrisation. Brannen (2006) showed that one can reconstruct the three masses from a single mass scale plus a 2/9-radian angle, by treating them as the eigenvalues of a 3×3 circulant matrix. The diagonalising matrix is the 3-point DFT, whose (j,k) entry is ω^{jk}/√3 with ω = exp(2πi/3). The fact that this structure is the cyclic group Z₃ of generations is what motivated Sheppeard's interpretation: every appearance of the 1/3, 1/√3, 2/3 numbers in flavour physics — including the tribimaximal MNS matrix — is a remnant of an underlying Z₃ Fourier structure. She and Brannen extended this to a sum of a 1-circulant (a + bω + cω²) and a 2-circulant (acting via ω↦ω²) to handle non-circulant unitaries, and conjectured the right symmetry to extend the construction to the CKM matrix is a non-abelian DFT — the project Brannen returned to in his 2021 paper "Spin Path Integrals and Generations" (viXra:2105.0146).

### Why her work is unconventional but defensible
The standard objection to Koide-type relations is that they are imposed at low energy where running of masses ought to make any simple relation accidental; Koide himself appeals to family symmetries (yukawaons) to protect the relation. Sheppeard's response, articulated repeatedly on her blogs and in her thesis, is that the relation is not a low-energy QFT effect at all but a fingerprint of a more fundamental quantum-gravitational origin in which mass arises from a categorical/motivic mapping rather than a Higgs-type mechanism — she famously called the Higgs the "fairy field," and after the 2012 Higgs discovery modified her models to accommodate (rather than predict) the 125 GeV resonance. From the orthodox vantage this is unfalsifiable; from a heterodox vantage it is one of the very few research programs that takes the empirical accuracy of the Koide relation seriously enough to build a theory around. (Yukinari Sumino, arXiv:0812.2103, gives the experimental accuracy explicitly: "The relative experimental error of the left-hand side of eq. (1) is dominated by ½(m_μ/m_τ)^½(Δm_τ/m_τ) and is of order 10⁻⁵.")

### What survives
The technically robust deliverables of her program are: (i) the explicit decomposition of the tribimaximal MNS matrix as a sum of 1- and 2-circulants whose entries are 12th roots of unity; (ii) the recognition that Brannen's eigenvalue parametrisation of Koide is identically a Z₃ DFT; (iii) the suggestion that the down-quark Brannen-Koide angle 4/27 = 2·(2/9)·(1/3) is related to the charged-lepton 2/9 via a triality of J₃(O); (iv) early advocacy (predating the amplituhedron) for the relevance of associahedra, motivic structures, and twistor polytopes to scattering amplitudes; (v) a categorical reformulation of Bilson-Thompson preon braids that includes "mirror" generations corresponding to braids unused in the original Bilson-Thompson scheme. Items (i)–(ii) are essentially mathematical observations and stand independently of her cosmological speculations; items (iii)–(v) are speculative but constitute a coherent research program.

## Recommendations

For a researcher wanting to build on or evaluate Sheppeard's work:

1. **Start with viXra:1711.0336 ("Lepton mass phases and the CKM matrix," 2017).** It is her most polished late synthesis and contains the core technical claims (2/9 lepton phase, the π/6 mirror-cancellation, the CKM phase decomposition, the fine-structure-constant estimate from Hopf-link Jones invariants at 5π/6) in compact form.

2. **Then read the 2007 thesis "Gluon Phenomenology and a Linear Topos"** (UC Research Repository handle 10092/1436). The phenomenology chapters are the link between the formal categorical material and the Brannen-Koide circulants.

3. **Read Brannen's "Koide mass equations for hadrons"** (brannenworks.com/koidehadrons.pdf) for the cleanest statement of the circulant decomposition of the tribimaximal MNS, with explicit acknowledgement of Sheppeard's DFT observation.

4. **For the categorical / amplitude-side material**, read the 2003 Crane–Sheppeard arXiv paper (math/0306440) and her 2014 viXra paper "The Associahedra and Permutohedra Yet Again." These can be evaluated as serious technical contributions independent of her phenomenological claims.

5. **For the historical/biographical and citation context**, "Notes on the work of Marni Sheppeard" (sheppeardnotes.wordpress.com) by Mitchell Porter is the single best secondary source, particularly the page "The lost thread" (2011) and the obituary "Information loss" (3 April 2021).

**Thresholds that would change a positive evaluation:** if a serious independent calculation showed that the Brannen–Sheppeard 4/27 down-quark angle does not survive QCD running of quark masses to a common scale, or that the proposed J₃(O) triality does not actually relate the 2/9 and 4/27 angles algebraically, this would significantly weaken the program. Conversely, if the new generation of work on categorical / motivic structure of scattering amplitudes (post-amplituhedron) explicitly recovered the kind of cyclic-group flavour structure she conjectured, this would vindicate her unusually early bet on motivic + Z₃ phenomenology.

## Caveats

- **Date of death**: The user's prompt placing her death "around 2014" is inaccurate. She remained active on viXra and Twitter through November 2020. Online concern about her destitution peaked in 2013 (e.g. "Save Kea!" on the Low Dimensional Topology blog) but she was alive then. Her remains were recovered near Otira/Arthur's Pass in late March 2021.
- **viXra is not peer-reviewed.** Almost all of her post-2007 output is on viXra, an unmoderated archive widely viewed as fringe. Gerard 't Hooft (Utrecht University, on his personal webpage staff.science.uu.nl/~hooft101/) wrote: "When a paper is published in viXra, it is usually a sign that it is not likely to contain acceptable results. It may, but the odds against that are considerable." This does not mean her papers are wrong, but they have not had refereed scrutiny, and the burden of evaluation falls on the reader.
- **Speculative components**: Her late cosmology — mirror-neutrino dark matter, Hawking-radiation origin of all matter from a cosmological horizon, Riofrio's varying-speed-of-light cosmology — is highly speculative, was rejected by the mainstream during her lifetime, and is not necessary for the technical Z₃/circulant/Koide observations to stand or fall.
- **Citation count is low.** ~111 citations total per Google Scholar. The Z₃-DFT recognition of Koide's structure was anticipated by P. F. Harrison and W. G. Scott, "Symmetries and Generalisations of Tri-Bimaximal Neutrino Mixing," Phys. Lett. B 535 (2002) 163 (arXiv:hep-ph/0203209), on circulant flavour matrices (note: the related three-author Harrison–Perkins–Scott paper proposing tribimaximal mixing itself is hep-ph/0202074, Phys. Lett. B 530 (2002) 167); priority disputes on the Brannen–Sheppeard side are partly avoidable because Brannen blog-posted the result in real time in 2006.
- **Personal context affects sourcing.** Many of her self-descriptions (e.g. ResearchGate bio: "still looking for a home, after over a decade of abuse and torture and twenty years of severe poverty") are unverifiable and reflect her own framing of her academic exile; they should be reported as her view rather than as established fact. The factual core — homelessness in Auckland in 2019, City Mission shelter, intermittent waitressing, prior 2003 mountaineering rescue — is well documented in mainstream New Zealand news (1News, Stuff, NZ Police press release of 25 February 2021) and in Tommaso Dorigo's Science 2.0 obituary.
- **Some specific paper IDs (viXra:0907.0011, 1004.0083, 1107.0002, 1010.0029) are inferred from citations and blog references rather than from a direct browse of her viXra author page** (which exists at vixra.org/author/m_d_sheppeard but could not be retrieved as readable text by the tools available); the IDs and approximate topics are reliable but a few titles may be slightly off. The total count of "approximately 28 viXra preprints" is the figure given by David Chester in his 2021 Quantum Gravity Research YouTube tribute "The Legacy of Dr. Marni Sheppeard"; an exact count would require a direct enumeration of the author page.
