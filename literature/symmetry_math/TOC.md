## A. Sheppeard authored / co-authored

- [Sheppeard viXra:1411.0587 (2014)](sheppeard_2014_mubs/one_pager.md) — "A Simple Lecture on MUBs."
- [Sheppeard viXra:1507.0169 (2015)](sheppeard_2015_algebras/one_pager.md) — "Lectures on Affine, Hyperbolic and Quantum Algebras."
- [Sheppeard viXra:1709.0035 (2017)](sheppeard_2017_phenomenology/one_pager.md) — first phenomenology paper after long hiatus.
- [Dungworth & Sheppeard viXra:1711.0119 (2017)](dungworth_sheppeard_2017_mirror/one_pager.md) — "Non Local Mirror Neutrinos with R=ct"; CMB/Koide 0.00117 eV coincidence.
- [Sheppeard viXra:1711.0336 (2017)](sheppeard_2017_lepton_phases/one_pager.md) — "Lepton mass phases and the CKM matrix"; her most polished late synthesis.
