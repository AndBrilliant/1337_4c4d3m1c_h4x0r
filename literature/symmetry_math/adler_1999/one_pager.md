# Adler 1999 — Discrete chiral symmetry origin of families

## Reference
S. L. Adler, *Model for particle masses, flavor mixing, and CP violation, based on spontaneously broken discrete chiral symmetry as the origin of families*, **arXiv:hep-ph/9806518** → *Phys. Rev. D* **59** (1999) 015012.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §5 / §10 (Discrete-flavor derivations).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Constructs a model of fermion masses, flavor mixing and CP violation in which the three families originate from a spontaneously-broken discrete chiral symmetry. The key technical device is the use of **retrocirculant Higgs structures**, which naturally produce Koide-like sum rules among the resulting masses. One of the earliest serious mechanism proposals that produces Koide-type structure as a consequence of a discrete-symmetry assumption.
