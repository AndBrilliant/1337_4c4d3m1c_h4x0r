# Brannen 2006 — MASSES.pdf, MASSES2.pdf — Cosine/circulant parametrization with δ ≈ 2/9

## Reference
C. A. Brannen, *Koide Mass Formula for Neutrinos* (brannenworks.com/MASSES.pdf, April 2006); *The Lepton Masses* (brannenworks.com/MASSES2.pdf, 2 May 2006). Bull. Am. Phys. Soc. (Brannen 2006, BAPS, APS NW meeting). Also viXra:0702.0052.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §B (Parametrizations and extensions). Identified as the **single most influential post-Koide reformulation**.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Brannen wrote the charged-lepton square-roots as eigenvalues of a real-eigenvalued circulant matrix:
- √m_n = μ[1 + √2 η cos(δ + 2πn/3)], n = 0,1,2
- Empirical fit: η² = 0.500003(23), i.e. η² = 1/2 ↔ Q = 2/3 *exactly*; the Koide constraint is automatic when η² = 1/2.
- δ ≈ 0.22222204717(48), strikingly close to **2/9** (the Brannen δ-coincidence).
- For neutrinos: same form with δ → δ + π/12, plus a sign flip on the lightest √m_ν, yielding μ_ν / μ_e = 1/3¹².

Brannen's δ ≈ 2/9 has driven much subsequent work (Żenczykowski 2012/2013/2018; G₂-Casimir conjectures; Sheppeard's entire late program). His preprints have circulated on physicsforums and viXra rather than arXiv (he had a brief arXiv listing that was withdrawn by moderators); they appear in INSPIRE only as "as cited in" through Rivero–Gsponer.

## Sections / contents
- Cosine/circulant parametrization √m_n = μ(1 + √2 η cos(δ + 2πn/3)).
- Constraint η² = 1/2 ⇔ Q = 2/3 (Koide automatic).
- Empirical δ ≈ 2/9 charged-lepton phase.
- Neutrino-Koide formula (sign flip on lightest √m_ν, δ → δ + π/12).
- Mechanism interpretation: circulant Hermitian mass matrix Γ(A,B,C); colored-preon framework.
