# Brannen 2021 — Spin Path Integrals and Generations (viXra:2105.0146)

## Reference
C. A. Brannen, *Spin Path Integrals and Generations*, **viXra:2105.0146** (May 2021).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §6 ("Discrete symmetries / Z₃ / Fourier picture"). Cited as Brannen's return to the non-Abelian DFT project that he and Sheppeard had conjectured to be the right symmetry to extend their construction to the CKM matrix.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Posted six months after Sheppeard's death. Returns to and develops the **non-Abelian / non-commutative DFT** that Sheppeard had identified (per Brannen 2021 Physics Forums note: "Marni Sheppeard recognized the way I redid the Koide formula as a Discrete Fourier Transform and supposed that what we needed was a Discrete Fourier Transform for a non-Abelian (well she said non commutative) symmetry") as the right framework to handle the CKM sector. Spin path integrals are the proposed mechanism connecting the non-Abelian DFT to family generations.

## Sections / contents
- Recap of the Brannen 2006 circulant Z₃ DFT for charged-lepton masses.
- Non-Abelian / non-commutative DFT framework.
- Spin path-integral construction.
- Application to family generations / CKM sector.
- Sheppeard memorial / posthumous credit.

## Status
viXra-only. Posthumous follow-up to the Brannen–Sheppeard collaboration. Closes the formal arc of the program.
