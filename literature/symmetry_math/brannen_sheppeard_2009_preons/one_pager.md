## Beyond-abstract summary
The only Brannen–Sheppeard joint arXiv paper. Couples Brannen's circulant-mass-matrix interpretation of Koide to a colored-preon model — a refinement of Bilson-Thompson's preon braid scheme in which the three Z₃-Fourier "colors" of the circulant are interpreted as a preon-color attribute. Provides the cleanest peer-adjacent venue for citing Sheppeard's contribution to the Z₃-DFT recognition.

## Sections / contents
- Brannen 2006 circulant parametrization with δ_L = 2/9 charged-lepton phase.
- Bilson-Thompson preon braid model with extended colored-preon assignment.
- Z₃-Fourier "color" interpretation of the three eigenvalues.
- Application to lepton and neutrino mass triplets.
