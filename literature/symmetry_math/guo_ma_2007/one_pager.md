# Guo & Ma 2007 — Foot's geometry connected to tribimaximal MNS

## Reference

Koide literature review research report, §12 (cross-sector / "universal" Koide proposals).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Connects Foot's 1994 geometric interpretation of the Koide relation (45° between √m vector and (1,1,1)) to the tribimaximal MNS matrix structure in the neutrino sector. Provides a geometric framing of why the same √m / equal-component-vector geometry that controls Koide also organises the tribimaximal mixing pattern.
