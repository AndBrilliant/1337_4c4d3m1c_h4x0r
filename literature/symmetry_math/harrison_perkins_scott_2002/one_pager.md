## Beyond-abstract summary
The paper that introduced the **tribimaximal MNS / PMNS mixing matrix** ansatz. Provides the unitary mixing-matrix structure that Sheppeard and Brannen later decomposed as the product of two quantum-Fourier operators (F₃F₂) on a qubit ⊗ qutrit Hilbert space. While not a Koide-mechanism paper, it is the empirical/structural anchor for the MNS-side of the Brannen–Sheppeard circulant program.

## Sections / contents
- Empirical neutrino mixing data circa 2002.
- Tribimaximal ansatz: |U_e1|² = 2/3, |U_e2|² = 1/3, |U_e3|² = 0; etc.
- Explicit form of the tribimaximal MNS matrix.
- Theoretical motivations for the ansatz.
