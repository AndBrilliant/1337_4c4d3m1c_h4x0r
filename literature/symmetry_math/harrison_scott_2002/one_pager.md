# Harrison & Scott 2002 — Symmetries and generalisations of tribimaximal mixing (Z₃-DFT priority)

## Reference
P. F. Harrison and W. G. Scott, *Symmetries and Generalisations of Tri-Bimaximal Neutrino Mixing*, **arXiv:hep-ph/0203209** → *Phys. Lett. B* **535** (2002) 163.

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, Caveats. Identified as a **priority precedent for Z₃-DFT recognition** in flavor matrices.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Generalises the Harrison–Perkins–Scott 2002 tribimaximal-mixing ansatz by analyzing its **symmetries** in terms of **circulant flavor matrices**. The biography report flags this as priority precedent: "The Z₃-DFT recognition of Koide's structure was anticipated by P. F. Harrison and W. G. Scott, *Symmetries and Generalisations of Tri-Bimaximal Neutrino Mixing*, *Phys. Lett. B* **535** (2002) 163, on circulant flavour matrices." The paper does not connect directly to Koide, but its circulant analysis is structurally identical to what Brannen and Sheppeard later applied to the lepton mass triplet.

## Sections / contents
- Symmetries of the tribimaximal MNS mixing matrix.
- Generalised tribimaximal ansätze.
- Circulant flavor matrices as the underlying structure.
- Implicit Z₃-DFT recognition (predates the Brannen-Sheppeard application by 4 years).
