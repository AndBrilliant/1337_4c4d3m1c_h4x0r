# Liang & Sun 2020 — Modified Koide from SU(3) flavor nonets

## Reference
Z. Liang and Z. Sun, *A modified version of the Koide formula from flavor nonets in a scalar potential model and in a Yukawaon model*, **arXiv:2007.05878** (2020–2021).

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §5 / §10 (Discrete-flavor / scalar-potential derivations).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Modern derivation of a modified Koide relation from **SU(3) flavor nonets**. In a scalar-potential model and a yukawaon model, the Yukawa nonet's VEV alignment produces a Koide-like sum rule with two effective parameters that fit the charged-lepton sector and both the up- and down-quark sectors simultaneously. Counted as the closest "pure symmetry" derivation candidate in the modern literature.
