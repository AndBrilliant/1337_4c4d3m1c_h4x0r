# Ma 2007 — Lepton family symmetry and possible application to the Koide mass formula

Koide literature review research report, §10 (Discrete-flavor derivations).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Uses a finite group generated by **four Z₃ transformations** to obtain mᵢ ∝ vᵢ², which is the structural prerequisite for the Koide relation. With mᵢ ∝ vᵢ² and a constraint on the VEVs vᵢ that fixes |⟨v⟩|² / Σvᵢ² = 2/3, the Koide relation follows. Demonstrates that a finite-group flavor symmetry can in principle deliver the Koide structure. Koide's hep-ph/0701018 ("A₄ symmetry") parallels this approach.
