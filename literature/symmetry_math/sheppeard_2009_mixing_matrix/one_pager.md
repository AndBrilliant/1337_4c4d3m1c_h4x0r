# Sheppeard 2009 — Mixing matrix paper (viXra:0907.0011)

## Reference
M. D. Sheppeard, **viXra:0907.0011** (July 2009). Linked as "mixing matrix" from her *Arcadian Functor* blog sidebar.

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4 ("viXra papers"). One of the early viXra papers in her ~28-paper output.

## Beyond-abstract summary
First substantive Sheppeard viXra contribution to the mixing-matrix problem after she lost arXiv endorsement. Presents the Brannen-style Z₃ Fourier reading of the lepton mass triplet and argues for a circulant decomposition of CKM/MNS — the line that culminated in viXra:1711.0336 in 2017.

## Sections / contents
- Setup of the 3×3 circulant mass-matrix formalism (continuation of Brannen 2006).
- Discrete Fourier transform on Z₃ acting on √m vector.
- Initial discussion of how the same construction applies to the mixing matrix sector.
- Connection to her categorical / ribbon-graph framework (linked via the *Arcadian Functor* blog).
