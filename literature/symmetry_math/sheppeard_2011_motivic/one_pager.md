# Sheppeard 2011 — Major motivic / categorical synthesis (viXra:1107.0002)

## Reference
M. D. Sheppeard, **viXra:1107.0002** (July 2011).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4 and Tom Copeland's November 2016 retrospective on *Arcadian Functor* / *Constructive Motives and Scattering*.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The most fully developed statement of Sheppeard's late research program. Outlines a canonical mapping from a category of measurement algebras (Schwinger / exceptional Jordan algebras) to a category of geometric objects (braids) via Grothendieck motives, which is argued to induce the Brannen-Koide circulant mass matrices as a structural consequence rather than an empirical input. Tom Copeland (Nov 2016): "It was Marni Sheppeard through her paper *Constructive Motives and Scattering* who first gave me a docent's tour of this tapestry" — the tapestry interweaving Grassmannians, associahedra, cluster algebras, MZVs, generalised permutohedra, and scattering amplitudes — well before Arkani-Hamed's amplituhedron paper appeared.

## Sections / contents
- Category of measurement algebras (Schwinger / J₃(O)).
- Category of braids / Bilson-Thompson preon structures.
- Grothendieck motives as the intermediating cohomological structure.
- Functor inducing Brannen-Koide circulant mass matrices.
- Connection to Grassmannian / associahedron / cluster-algebra / MZV / permutohedron structure of scattering amplitudes (predates Arkani-Hamed amplituhedron).

## Status
viXra-only. Cited by Tom Copeland's blog as a precursor to the amplituhedron program; cited by Brannen's website. The single most ambitious Sheppeard paper after the PhD thesis. Speculative; technically dense.
