# Sheppeard 2014 — A Simple Lecture on MUBs (viXra:1411.0587)

## Reference
M. D. Sheppeard, *A Simple Lecture on MUBs*, **viXra:1411.0587** (November 2014).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Pedagogical introduction to mutually unbiased bases (MUBs) for Hilbert spaces of dimension p^n. Provides the quantum-information rationale that Sheppeard used to motivate the appearance of "magic" matrices in flavor physics — namely that the tribimaximal MNS form is naturally expressible as a product of Fourier operators on a qubit ⊗ qutrit Hilbert space (p=2 ⊗ p=3, dimension 6).

## Sections / contents
- Definition of mutually unbiased bases (MUBs); the p^n MUB existence theorem.
- Discrete Fourier transform on Z_p as the canonical pair of MUBs.
- Tensor product MUB structure for prime-power dimensions.
- Application: F₃F₂ decomposition of the tribimaximal MNS matrix as a 6-dimensional MUB-related operator.

## Status
viXra-only, lecture-style. The MUB material is standard quantum-information theory; the application to flavor physics / circulant mass matrices is Sheppeard's distinctive contribution.
