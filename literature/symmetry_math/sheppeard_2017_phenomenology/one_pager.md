## Beyond-abstract summary
Sheppeard's return to active phenomenological work after a 2014–2017 gap dominated by personal hardship (the period including her 2018 Group32 talk in Prague, her trip to the USA, and the eventual 2019 homelessness). Sets up the technical machinery later refined in viXra:1711.0336.

- 2/9 lepton phase reaffirmed in light of post-2014 PDG values.
- Preliminary CKM-phase decomposition material.
- Setup for the explicit α / fine-structure-constant estimate from Hopf-link Jones invariants that appears in viXra:1711.0336.
