Sheppeard biography research report, §3 ("PhD thesis"). The recommended second-read after viXra:1711.0336.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Applies category theory and topos theory to QFT observables. Develops an "abstract categorical Machian principle of duality for a ribbon graph calculus" intended to incorporate Carl Brannen's Koide-type mass calculations and Sundance Bilson-Thompson's preon braid scheme. Derives Veneziano-type n-point functions using cohomological techniques inspired by twistor theory and MHV amplitudes. Gives "axioms for a quantum topos." It is unusual in combining a high-formal-machinery thesis (categorical / topos) with a phenomenology chapter on Koide circulants — the signature of her later viXra program.

## Sections / contents
- Categorical foundations / topos theory for QFT observables (in the line of Chris Isham).
- Machian duality principle for a ribbon-graph calculus.
- Bilson-Thompson preon braids decorated with Brannen-Koide circulant phase data.
- Cohomological / twistor / MHV-amplitude derivation of Veneziano-type n-point functions.
- Axioms for a quantum topos.
- Phenomenology chapter linking categorical machinery to Brannen-Koide mass matrices.
