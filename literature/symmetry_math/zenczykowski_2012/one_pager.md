## Beyond-abstract summary
Sharpens the Brannen Z₃ phase observation. Argues that the empirical low-energy phase parameters obey **δ_L = 3δ_D/2 = 3δ_U = 2/9**, with k_L = 1, where δ_L is the charged-lepton Brannen phase and δ_U, δ_D are the corresponding up-quark and down-quark phases. Establishes that δ_L = 2/9 is empirically indistinguishable from the exact value.
