## Beyond-abstract summary
Follow-up to Żenczykowski 2012 (PRD 86 117303). Extends the Z₃-symmetric parametrization analysis to **quark mixings** as well as masses, refining the Brannen-Sheppeard quark phases using Goffinet (2008) "pseudo-masses" rather than naive √m. Strengthens the empirical case for δ_L = 2/9, δ_U = 2/27, δ_D = 4/27.

## Sections / contents

- Goffinet pseudo-mass refinement.
- Application to quark mass matrices and CKM mixings.
- Sum rule δ_L + δ_ν = δ_U + δ_D.
