## Source
§4 (Z₂ symmetry), tier ✦ — K-theoretic periodic table.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Builds the K-theoretic periodic table of gapped free-fermion phases by combining the Altland–Zirnbauer ten symmetry classes with Bott periodicity ($\pi_0(C_q)$ for complex K-theory, $\pi_0(R_q)$ for real). The classifying spaces $C_q$ (period 2) and $R_q$ (period 8) give topological invariants $0$, $\mathbb{Z}$, or $\mathbb{Z}_2$ depending on symmetry and dimension. Examples: IQHE = $\mathbb{Z}$ (class A, d=2); Majorana chain = $\mathbb{Z}_2$ (no symmetry, d=1); Bi$_{1-x}$Sb$_x$ 3D TI = $\mathbb{Z}_2$ (T+Q, d=3). The $\mathbb{Z}_2$ invariants come from Clifford-algebra extension problems, with particle–hole as a positive-square involution.
