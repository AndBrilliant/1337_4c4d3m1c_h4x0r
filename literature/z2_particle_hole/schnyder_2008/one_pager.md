## Beyond-abstract summary
Systematic classification of single-particle gapped Hamiltonians in 3D by time-reversal $T$, particle–hole $C$, and chiral $S = TC$ symmetries, completing the Altland–Zirnbauer tenfold way. Five of the ten AZ symmetry classes (AIII, BDI, CII, CI, DIII) support nontrivial 3D topological phases characterized by integer winding numbers; the remaining classes are trivial in 3D. The work establishes the canonical mapping AZ class ↔ symmetric space ↔ K-theory invariant that the Kitaev (2009) periodic table extends via Bott periodicity. Particle–hole symmetry enters here as the involutory $Z_2$ structure that defines the BdG and chiral classes.
