> §8 of the source report: *Scale-free / RG-invariant mass ratios.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: the cascade ratio $R_K = 1/\alpha_K^2 = 19.798$ predicts $m_s/m_d$, which is RG-invariant under QCD because $\gamma_m$ is flavor-independent. This subfolder collects the canonical citations for that fact, plus the CM analog (universal amplitude ratios at a Wilson–Fisher fixed point).

## Tier 1 (★ — canonical citation)

- [Leutwyler 1996](leutwyler_1996/one_pager.md) — *The ratios of the light quark masses,* Phys. Lett. B 378 (1996) 313 [arXiv:hep-ph/9602366]. m_u/m_d = 0.553 ± 0.043 and m_s/m_d = 18.9 ± 0.8 are RG-invariant pure numbers.

## Tier 1 (★ — foundational review)

- [Gasser & Leutwyler 1982](gasser_leutwyler_1982/one_pager.md) — *Quark masses,* Phys. Rep. 87 (1982) 77. Foundational review of RG running of quark masses and scheme-independence of ratios.

## Tier 1 (★ — CM analog: universal amplitude ratios)

- [Pelissetto & Vicari 2002](pelissetto_vicari_2002/one_pager.md) — *Critical phenomena and renormalization-group theory,* Phys. Rep. 368 (2002) 549 [arXiv:cond-mat/0012164]. CM master reference for universal amplitude ratios at the Wilson–Fisher fixed point.

## Tier 1 (★ — modern CM RG-invariant ratios)

- [Jüttner, Litim & Marchais 2017](juttner_litim_marchais_2017/one_pager.md) — *Global Wilson–Fisher fixed points,* Nucl. Phys. B 921 (2017) 769 [arXiv:1701.05168]. O(N) universal exponents and mass ratios at the WF fixed point.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Wilson & Kogut 1974](wilson_kogut_1974/one_pager.md) — *The renormalization group and the ε expansion,* Phys. Rep. 12 (1974) 75. Original RG-with-universal-ratios reference.
