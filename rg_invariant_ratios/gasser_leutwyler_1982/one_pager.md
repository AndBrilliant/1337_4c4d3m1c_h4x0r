# Gasser & Leutwyler 1982 — Quark masses

## Reference
J. Gasser, H. Leutwyler, *Quark masses*, **Phys. Rep. 87 (1982) 77–169**. No arXiv (1982).

## Source
§8 (RG-invariant mass ratios), tier ★ — foundational review.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The foundational 93-page Physics Reports review establishing the modern framework for light-quark mass determination via chiral perturbation theory. Demonstrates: (i) the existence of well-defined "current quark masses" via SU(3) chiral symmetry breaking; (ii) the leading-order Gell-Mann–Oakes–Renner relations $M_{\pi^+}^2 = (m_u + m_d)B$, $M_{K^+}^2 = (m_u + m_s)B$ etc.; (iii) the *flavor-independence* of the QCD mass anomalous dimension $\gamma_m$, which makes mass *ratios* scheme- and scale-independent. The work is the textbook reference for "running quark masses can be defined; their ratios are RG invariants."

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Cited together with Leutwyler 1996 as the foundational reference for "$m_s/m_d$ is a scale-free pure number" — the theoretical underpinning of the F-identity paper's prediction $R_K = m_s/m_d \approx 19.8$ being comparable to PDG/FLAG values without explicit scale specification.
