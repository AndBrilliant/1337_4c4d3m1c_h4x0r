# Jüttner, Litim & Marchais 2017 — Global Wilson–Fisher fixed points

## Reference
A. Jüttner, D. F. Litim, E. Marchais, *Global Wilson–Fisher fixed points*, **Nucl. Phys. B 921 (2017) 769–795**; **arXiv:1701.05168**.

## Source
§8 (RG-invariant mass ratios), tier ★ — modern computation of universal exponents and mass ratios at the WF fixed point.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Uses the functional renormalization group (FRG) to compute the global Wilson–Fisher fixed-point solution of O(N) scalar field theory across all N from N=1 to N=∞, including local potential approximation results for: critical exponents (η, ν, ω), universal amplitude ratios, and *mass ratios* in symmetry-broken phases. The work establishes that at any WF fixed point, ratios of physical mass scales (e.g., the mass gap of one channel to another) are pure numbers determined by N alone. The closest modern technical reference for "RG-invariant mass ratio from CM fixed-point physics."

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Cited (with Pelissetto–Vicari 2002 and Leutwyler 1996) when establishing that the F-identity manuscript's mass-ratio prediction $R_K = m_s/m_d$ has a quantitative CM precedent — not just a qualitative analogy. If a referee asks "what is the modern CM literature on RG-invariant mass ratios?", Jüttner et al. 2017 is the recent technical answer.
