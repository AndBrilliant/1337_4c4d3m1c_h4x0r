## Beyond-abstract summary
A 180-page Physics Reports review of critical phenomena and Wilson-style renormalization-group theory. The framework relevant to the F-identity manuscript: at a critical point (Wilson–Fisher fixed point), critical exponents *and* universal amplitude ratios — dimensionless combinations of correlation amplitudes — depend only on the universality class (symmetry + dimensionality), not on microscopic details. Tabulates ratios like $A^+/A^-$ (specific heat above/below $T_c$), $R_\xi^+$, $R_C^+$ across O(N) universality classes. Universal amplitude ratios are the CM analog of RG-invariant quark mass ratios: scale-free dimensionless numbers fixed by the fixed-point physics.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
The single best CM analogy for "RG-invariant mass ratio from a fixed-point structure." Cited in §1/Discussion when establishing that the F-identity manuscript's geometric ratio $R_K = m_s/m_d = 19.8$ has a direct CM analog in the universal amplitude ratios at the Wilson–Fisher fixed point.
