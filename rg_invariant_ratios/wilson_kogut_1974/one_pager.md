# Wilson & Kogut 1974 — The renormalization group and the ε expansion

## Reference
K. G. Wilson, J. Kogut, *The renormalization group and the ε expansion*, **Phys. Rep. 12 (1974) 75–199**. No arXiv (1974).

## Source
§8 (RG-invariant mass ratios), tier ✦ — the original RG-with-universal-ratios reference.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The foundational Physics Reports review of the Wilson renormalization group and the ε-expansion. Establishes: (i) the concept of a *fixed point* of the RG flow; (ii) the eigenvalues of the linearized flow as the critical exponents; (iii) universality — multiple microscopic models flowing to the same fixed point have identical macroscopic critical behavior. This is the foundational paper for *both* the modern CM treatment of critical phenomena and the modern theoretical-physics conception of universality classes. The notion of "RG-invariant ratio" — a dimensionless combination of physical quantities that depends only on the fixed point — originates here.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Foundational citation for the entire "RG fixed-point ↔ universal ratio" framework. If the F-identity manuscript's geometric ratio $R_K = m_s/m_d$ is to be interpreted as a fixed-point output (rather than a coincidence), Wilson–Kogut 1974 is the citation establishing that "ratios fixed by RG flow" is a legitimate physical concept.
