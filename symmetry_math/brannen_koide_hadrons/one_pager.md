# Brannen — Koide mass equations for hadrons

## Reference
C. A. Brannen, *Koide mass equations for hadrons*, hosted at brannenworks.com/koidehadrons.pdf.

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §5 (recommended reading item 3) and Recommendations.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The cleanest extant statement of the **circulant decomposition of the tribimaximal MNS matrix**, with explicit acknowledgement of Sheppeard's DFT observation. Extends the circulant / Z₃-DFT framework from leptons to hadron masses, using the same √m → eigenvalue-of-circulant ansatz applied to baryon and meson mass triplets. Refers to a forthcoming joint Brannen–Sheppeard paper on the CKM and MNS matrices as 1-circulant + 2-circulant sums.

## Sections / contents
- Recap of charged-lepton Brannen-Koide circulant with δ_L = 2/9.
- Application to hadron mass triplets.
- Tribimaximal MNS as 1-circulant + 2-circulant sum (with (1/3, 2/3) normalization).
- Acknowledgement of Sheppeard for the Z₃-DFT recognition.
- Forward-pointer to the joint Brannen–Sheppeard DFT paper.
