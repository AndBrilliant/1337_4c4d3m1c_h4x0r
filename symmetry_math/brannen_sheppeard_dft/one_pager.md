# Brannen & Sheppeard — Discrete Fourier Transform and Particle Mixing Matrices

## Reference
C. A. Brannen and M. D. Sheppeard, *The Discrete Fourier Transform and the Particle Mixing Matrices*. Hosted at brannenworks.com/Gravity/PRD_NewParamRefs.pdf. The URL stub suggests an unsuccessful Phys. Rev. D submission.

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §5 ("Carl Brannen collaboration"). The seminal joint preprint of the Brannen–Sheppeard collaboration.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Argues that the CKM and MNS matrices are best decomposed as **sums of 1-circulant and 2-circulant 3×3 matrices**, with the tribimaximal MNS form admitting an especially clean (1/3, 2/3) decomposition. Brannen wrote in 2021 on Physics Forums: "Marni Sheppeard recognized the way I redid the Koide formula as a Discrete Fourier Transform and supposed that what we needed was a Discrete Fourier Transform for a non-Abelian (well she said non commutative) symmetry." Brannen's *Koide mass equations for hadrons* paper credits this paper for the DFT observation and refers to it as a forthcoming joint paper.

## Sections / contents
- Recognition that Brannen's 2006 cosine parametrization √mₖ = √μ(1 + √2 cos(δ + 2πk/3)) is identically a 3-point DFT (Fourier on Z₃).
- Decomposition of unitary 3×3 matrices as sums of 1-circulant (a + bω + cω²) and 2-circulant (acting via ω↦ω²) parts.
- Application to the tribimaximal MNS matrix: clean (1/3, 2/3) split.
- Application to the CKM matrix: perturbation of a circulant + 2-circulant decomposition involving 12th roots of unity.
- Conjecture: the right symmetry to extend to CKM is a non-Abelian DFT — the project Brannen returned to in viXra:2105.0146 (2021).

## Status
Personal-website preprint, not arXiv. Apparently submitted to Phys. Rev. D (per URL stub) but not accepted. Counted as the joint deliverable of the Brannen–Sheppeard collaboration; cited in Brannen's later viXra papers and on his website.
