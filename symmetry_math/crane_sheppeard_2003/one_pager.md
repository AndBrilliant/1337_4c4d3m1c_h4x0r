## Beyond-abstract summary
Builds Lie 2-group representation theory aimed at a Poincaré 2-category state-sum model of 4D quantum gravity. Sheppeard listed with affiliation "Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Canterbury, Christchurch, New Zealand." Co-author Louis Crane was at Kansas State / Perimeter Institute. Important as the formal-categorical foundation that Sheppeard later carried forward into her PhD thesis and her viXra program.

## Sections / contents
- Construction of 2-categorical representations of the Poincaré 2-group.
- State-sum applications (4D quantum-gravity oriented).
- Setup that Sheppeard's later work treats as the natural arena for ribbon-graph / preon / mass-matrix structure.
