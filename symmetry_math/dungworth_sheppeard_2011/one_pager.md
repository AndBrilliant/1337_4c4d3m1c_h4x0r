# Dungworth & Sheppeard 2011 — First joint paper on neutrino/CMB mass relations (viXra:1102.0010)

## Reference
G. Dungworth and M. D. Sheppeard, **viXra:1102.0010** (February 2011).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4. Identified as the first joint Dungworth–Sheppeard paper.

## Beyond-abstract summary
First joint paper between Sheppeard and her cosmology collaborator Graham Dungworth. Establishes the line of work — extended six years later in viXra:1711.0119 — that connects the Brannen-Koide neutrino mass triplet (with √m_ν1 → −√m_ν1) to a CMB-temperature-related mass scale. The follow-up identifies the central mirror-neutrino mass under the Koide relation as 0.00117 eV, coincident with k_BT_CMB/c².

## Sections / contents
- Brannen 2006 neutrino-Koide formula recap (sign flip on √m_ν1; μ_ν / μ_e = 1/3¹²).
- Mirror neutrino structure and CMB mass scale.
- Cosmological framing (precedes the explicit R = ct setup of the 2017 paper).

## Status
viXra-only. Heterodox cosmology framework; the technical Brannen-Koide neutrino content is independent of the cosmological speculation and stands or falls with the Brannen formula itself.
