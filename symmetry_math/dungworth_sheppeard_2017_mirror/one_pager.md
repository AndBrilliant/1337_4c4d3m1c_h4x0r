# Dungworth & Sheppeard 2017 — Non Local Mirror Neutrinos with R=ct (viXra:1711.0119)

## Reference
G. Dungworth and M. D. Sheppeard, *Non Local Mirror Neutrinos with R=ct*, **viXra:1711.0119** (November 2017).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4. Cited explicitly for the CMB / 0.00117 eV coincidence.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Notes the coincidence between the CMB temperature and the central mirror-neutrino mass under the Koide relation: **0.00117 eV**, equal to k_BT_CMB/c². Embeds the result in a "non-local mirror neutrino" framework with R = ct cosmology (Riofrio-style varying-c). The 0.00117 eV scale is independently relevant for LSND / MiniBooNE anomalies and for superfluid-MOND dark matter (Khoury–Berezhiani).

## Sections / contents
- Brannen 2006 neutrino-Koide formula with sign flip on √m_ν1.
- Calculation of the central neutrino-mass scale (~0.00117 eV) under Koide.
- Identification with k_BT_CMB/c².
- Embedding in the R = ct cosmology framework.
- Speculation on connection to LSND / MiniBooNE anomalies and superfluid-MOND dark matter.

## Status
viXra-only. Heterodox cosmology framework; 0.00117 eV coincidence is independent of the cosmology and is the technical takeaway. A 2018 follow-up paper derives a 1 eV neutrino mass scale by multiplying the 0.00117 eV scale by an appropriate factor.
