# Esposito & Santorelli 1995 — A Geometric Picture for Fermion Masses

## Reference
S. Esposito and P. Santorelli, *A Geometric Picture for Fermion Masses*, **arXiv:hep-ph/9603369** → *Mod. Phys. Lett. A* **10** (1995) 3077.

## Source
Koide literature review research report, §D (Geometric and group-theoretic readings). Identified as the predecessor to Kocik's 2012 Descartes-circle reading.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Pre-Foot paper proposing a geometric reading of fermion mass spectra. Shows that Koide-like relations can be expressed as constraints on a "fermion mass vector" in a geometric setting that is RG-invariant below the electroweak scale. While Foot 1994 became the canonical 45° geometric statement, Esposito–Santorelli's contribution is the earliest geometric reframing in the journal literature.

## Sections / contents
- Geometric setup with √m or m vectors.
- Constraints from observed mass hierarchies.
- RG-invariance properties below electroweak scale.
- Connection to Koide-type sum rules.
