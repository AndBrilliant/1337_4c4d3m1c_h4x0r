## Beyond-abstract summary
Extends the knot/preon program (fermions as quantum knots described by SL_q(2) irreps) to the strong sector by introducing SU(3) color charges. Continuation of Finkelstein's electroweak knot-preon work, not part of the Brannen Koide-formula line. Listed here for completeness because the misnamed file lived under symmetry_math; do not confuse with Brannen-Sheppeard 2009 "Colored Preons" (which has its own directory).

## Sections / contents
- SL_q(2) knot description of fermions reviewed.
- SU(3) color charge introduction.
- Compatibility with the standard electroweak knot-preon framework.
