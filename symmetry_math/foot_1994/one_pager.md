# Foot 1994 — A note on Koide's lepton mass relation

## Reference
R. Foot, *A note on Koide's lepton mass relation*, **arXiv:hep-ph/9402242** (1994). McGill preprint, never journal-published.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §4 (Geometric approaches) and §3 (geometric reading).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Showed that the Koide relation Q = (mₑ+m_μ+m_τ)/(√mₑ+√m_μ+√m_τ)² = 2/3 is equivalent to the angle between (√mₑ, √m_μ, √m_τ) and (1,1,1) being exactly **π/4 (45°)** — equivalently cos²θ = 1/2. This 45° mnemonic is now used by virtually every subsequent paper on Koide-type relations. Foot was cautious about interpretation: "It could be that the mass formula eq.(1) is of no fundamental significance since it is possible that its empirical success is just a numerical coincidence." Observation, not mechanism. Has not pursued a discrete-group derivation; his mirror-symmetry program is on a separate track.

## Sections / contents

- Equivalent formulation as cos²θ = 1/2.
- Discussion of whether the relation is fundamental or coincidence.
