# Gibbs 2009 — 3×3 Unitary to Magic Matrix Transformations

## Reference
P. Gibbs, *3×3 Unitary to Magic Matrix Transformations*, **viXra:0907.0002** (2009).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §9 (Citations and reception).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Authored by Philip Gibbs, founder of viXra, this preprint was written **specifically motivated by a problem posed by Brannen and Sheppeard** in the early stages of their Discrete-Fourier-Transform program. Studies the transformations between general 3×3 unitary matrices and "magic" matrices (matrices with all rows, columns, and diagonals summing to the same constant) — exactly the structure that arises in tribimaximal MNS, in 1-circulant + 2-circulant decompositions, and in the Brannen–Sheppeard reformulation of CKM/MNS.

## Sections / contents
- Definition of magic matrices and their relation to unitarity.
- Transformations between 3×3 unitary matrices and magic-matrix forms.
- Application to tribimaximal-style mixing matrices.
- Acknowledgement of the Brannen–Sheppeard problem.

## Status
viXra:0907.0002, 2009. Counts as one of the few external citations of Sheppeard's program (alongside Brannen, Żenczykowski, Copeland). Useful technical reference for the magic-matrix/unitary transformations underlying the circulant decompositions.
