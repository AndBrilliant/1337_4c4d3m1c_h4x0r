# Goffinet 2008 — A bottom-up approach to fermion masses

## Reference
F. Goffinet, *A bottom-up approach to fermion masses*, PhD thesis, Université catholique de Louvain (UCLouvain), 2008.

## Source
Both Koide literature review research reports, §5 / §12. The Sheppeard biography report notes Żenczykowski 2012/2013 used Goffinet "pseudo-masses" to refine the Brannen–Sheppeard quark phases.

## Beyond-abstract summary
PhD thesis treating Koide-type relations using **pole masses** rather than running masses, and offering a **cubic reformulation that avoids square roots** entirely (Goffinet "pseudo-masses"). The pseudo-mass framework is the technical foundation for Żenczykowski's later refinement of the quark Z₃ phases, and is the recommended technical machinery in any modern attempt to apply Koide-type relations across sectors without numerical artifacts from sign choices on √m.

## Sections / contents
- Bottom-up phenomenological framework for fermion masses.
- Pseudo-mass cubic reformulation: Koide-equivalent constraint without √m.
- Pole-mass treatment (avoids RG-running ambiguity).
- Application to lepton and quark sectors.
- Predecessor to the four-sector treatment in Gérard-Goffinet-Herquet 2006 (PLB 633 563).

## Status
PhD thesis, UCLouvain 2008. Cited as the technical machinery underlying Żenczykowski's Z₃-symmetric quark-mass refinement (PRD 86 / PRD 87, 2012/2013).
