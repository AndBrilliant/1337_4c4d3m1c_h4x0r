## Beyond-abstract summary
Reads the Koide relation as a generalized **Descartes–Apollonius circle theorem**. In the standard Descartes formula (a+b+c+d)² = 2(a²+b²+c²+d²) the coefficient is 2; reading curvatures bᵢ = 1/√mᵢ and dropping the fourth circle gives Koide's relation with coefficient 2/3. Equivalently, three coplanar circles meeting pairwise at the same angle φ = arccos(2/3) ≈ 48.2°. The paper also gives an n-dimensional generalization to n+2 spheres satisfying Σbᵢ² = (p/((n+1)p−1))(Σbᵢ)², with κ = 2/3 ⇒ p = 2/(2n−1). Accommodates Brannen's neutrino sign flip via negative curvature (unbounded disks).
