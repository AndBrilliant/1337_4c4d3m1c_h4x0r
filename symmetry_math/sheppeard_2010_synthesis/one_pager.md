# Sheppeard 2010 — Synthesis paper with 4/27 down-quark derivation (viXra:1010.0029)

## Reference
M. D. Sheppeard, **viXra:1010.0029** (October 2010).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4. Identified as the paper containing the page-17 derivation of the 4/27 down-quark angle.

## Beyond-abstract summary
The most technically distinctive single Sheppeard preprint. Contains, on its page 17, the derivation of the Brannen-Koide angle of **4/27 radians for down quarks** via a **triality transformation acting on the 2/9-radian charged-lepton angle** in the relevant exceptional Jordan algebra J₃(O). Mirror-neutrino factors of 2/9 cancel between left and right components to leave a residual π/6 phase (R³ = iI in the Cayley B₃ representation). This is the prototype of her later viXra:1711.0336 synthesis and is one of the five technically robust deliverables of her Koide program (item iii in the biography report's *Details* section).

## Sections / contents
- Review of Brannen 2006 cosine parametrization with δ_L = 2/9 for charged leptons.
- Setup of the Cayley B₃ / J₃(O) representation framework.
- Triality transformation acting on the 2/9 lepton phase.
- Mirror-neutrino / left-right cancellation.
- Result: down-quark angle δ_D = 4/27, related as 4/27 = 2·(2/9)·(1/3).
- Integration with the extended Bilson-Thompson mapping of viXra:1004.0083.
