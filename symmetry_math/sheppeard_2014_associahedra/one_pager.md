# Sheppeard 2014 — The Associahedra and Permutohedra Yet Again (viXra:1406.0125)

## Reference
M. D. Sheppeard, *The Associahedra and Permutohedra Yet Again*, **viXra:1406.0125** (June 2014).

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4.

## Beyond-abstract summary
Argues that Stasheff's associahedra (the operad of binary trees / planar tree composition) underlie tree-level scattering and connect to the permutohedra via cube tilings. The paper is technically conventional in its operadic content (does not require viXra-specific framework) and is one of the four Sheppeard contributions the biography report recommends evaluating independently of her phenomenological claims.

## Sections / contents
- Stasheff associahedron K_n review (binary trees / planar tree composition).
- Permutohedra and their cube-tiling refinements.
- Application to tree-level scattering amplitude structure.
- Connection to the operadic / motivic program Sheppeard had been developing since viXra:1107.0002.

## Status
viXra-only. The operadic content is closer to mainstream mathematics (Stasheff, Loday, Tonks) than to her speculative cosmology and can be evaluated as a serious technical contribution on its own terms. Counts as a precursor of the now-standard application of polytopes / cluster algebras to scattering amplitudes.
