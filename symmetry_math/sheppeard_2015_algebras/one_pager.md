## Beyond-abstract summary
A pedagogical lecture-format paper covering affine Lie algebras, hyperbolic Kac-Moody algebras, and quantum-group deformations. Provides the algebraic toolkit Sheppeard used in her categorical / motivic program — specifically the q-deformed Z₃ Fourier transform that underlies her ribbon-graph calculus.

- Hyperbolic Kac-Moody algebras (rank ≥ 3 indefinite cases).
- Quantum-group deformations U_q(g).
- Quantum Fourier transform / quantum dimension formulas.
- Implicit / exposition-level connection to her flavor work via q-deformed circulants.
