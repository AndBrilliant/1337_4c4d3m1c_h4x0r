# Sheppeard 2017 — Lepton mass phases and the CKM matrix (viXra:1711.0336)

## Reference

## Source
Sheppeard biography research report, §4 and Recommendations (item 1: the recommended starting point for evaluating Sheppeard's work).

## Beyond-abstract summary
Sheppeard's most polished late synthesis. Verbatim abstract: "The Brannen neutrino mass triplet extends Koide's rule for the charged leptons, which was used to correctly predict the τ mass. Assuming that Koide's rule is exact, we consider the fundamental 2/9 lepton phase, noting connections to the CKM matrix and arithmetic information. An estimate for the fine structure constant α is included." Contains the core technical claims of her late program: (i) the 2/9 charged-lepton phase as a fundamental invariant; (ii) the π/6 mirror-cancellation; (iii) the CKM phase decomposition; (iv) an estimate of α from Hopf-link Jones invariants at 5π/6.
