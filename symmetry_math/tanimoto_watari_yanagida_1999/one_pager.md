# Tanimoto, Watari & Yanagida 1999 — Broken O(3)_L × O(3)_R democratic mass matrices

## Reference

## Beyond-abstract summary
The closest stringy/SUSY environment to the discrete-flavor / democratic mass-matrix line. Constructs broken **O(3)_L × O(3)_R democratic mass matrices** for fermion masses, building on the democratic-mass-matrix tradition that Koide (1989, 1995, 1999) had been developing. While the paper does not derive the Koide relation directly, it provides a stringy/SUSY-compatible context within which Koide-type sum rules can naturally appear.
