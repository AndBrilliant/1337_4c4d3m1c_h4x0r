# z2_particle_hole/ — Table of Contents

> §4 of the source report: *Z₂ symmetry as particle–hole / matter–antimatter.*
> Hook into the F-identity manuscript: §2 reads $C_3 \times Z_2$ as the symmetry group, with $Z_2$ distinguishing particle from antiparticle; §6 takes $2m_e$ as the pair-production threshold (the $Z_2$ base unit). This subfolder collects the CM Z₂-classification literature.

## Tier 1 (★ — founding CM paper)

- [Kane & Mele 2005](kane_mele_2005/one_pager.md) — *Z₂ topological order and the quantum spin Hall effect,* PRL 95 (2005) 146802. Founding paper for the Kane–Mele Z₂ invariant.

## Tier 1 (★ — tenfold-way classification)

- [Schnyder, Ryu, Furusaki & Ludwig 2008](schnyder_2008/one_pager.md) — PRB 78 (2008) 195125 [arXiv:0803.2786]. Tenfold-way symmetry classification of single-particle Hamiltonians; particle–hole as Z₂ involution.

## Tier 1 (★ — Altland–Zirnbauer original)

- [Altland & Zirnbauer 1997](altland_zirnbauer_1997/one_pager.md) — PRB 55 (1997) 1142. Original ten symmetry classes with particle–hole as Z₂.

## Supporting (✦)

- [Kitaev 2009](kitaev_2009/one_pager.md) — *Periodic table for topological insulators,* AIP Conf. Proc. 1134 (2009) 22 [arXiv:0901.2686]. K-theoretic periodic table with Z₂ entries.
- [Fu & Kane 2007](fu_kane_2007/one_pager.md) — *Topological insulators with inversion symmetry,* PRB 76 (2007) 045302. Parity-based formula for the Z₂ invariant.
