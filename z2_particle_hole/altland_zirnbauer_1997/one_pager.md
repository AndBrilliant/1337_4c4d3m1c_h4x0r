## Beyond-abstract summary
Extends Dyson's threefold-way (A/GUE, AI/GOE, AII/GSE) to ten symmetry classes by including charge-conjugation (particle–hole, "C") and chiral ("S") symmetries. The four classes with $C$ alone or together with $T$ (BdG classes C, CI, D, DIII) plus the three chiral classes (AIII, BDI, CII) bring the count from 3 to 10. Each class corresponds to a Cartan symmetric space (A, AI, AII, AIII, BDI, CII, C, CI, D, DIII). Particle–hole symmetry appears as an antiunitary involution squaring to ±1 — the canonical $Z_2$-valued structure that defines superconductor/normal hybrid systems. The bijection between disordered single-particle universality classes and Cartan's symmetric spaces is the foundational result.
