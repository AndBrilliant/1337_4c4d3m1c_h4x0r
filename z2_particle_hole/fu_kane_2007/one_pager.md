# Fu & Kane 2007 — Topological insulators with inversion symmetry

## Reference
L. Fu, C. L. Kane, *Topological insulators with inversion symmetry*, **Phys. Rev. B 76 (2007) 045302**; **arXiv:cond-mat/0611341**.

## Source
§4 (Z₂ symmetry), tier ✦ — parity-based formula for the $Z_2$ invariant.

## Beyond-abstract summary
For crystals with both time-reversal symmetry $\Theta$ and inversion symmetry $P$, the strong $Z_2$ topological invariant $\nu_0$ equals the product of parity eigenvalues $\xi_n(\Gamma_i) = \pm 1$ of the occupied Kramers-degenerate bands at the eight time-reversal-invariant momenta $\Gamma_i$ of the 3D Brillouin zone: $(-1)^{\nu_0} = \prod_{i=1}^{8} \delta_i$ with $\delta_i = \prod_n \xi_n(\Gamma_i)$. Three "weak" invariants $\nu_1, \nu_2, \nu_3$ follow analogously. The parity-eigenvalue test predicts Bi$_{1-x}$Sb$_x$ to be a strong topological insulator, strained Sn and HgTe to be strong TIs, and Pb$_{1-x}$Sn$_x$Te to host an STI phase near the band inversion. The construction reduces $Z_2$ from an integral over Bloch wavefunctions to a finite tabulation of parity eigenvalues.

## Use in F-identity manuscript
Concrete CM example of a $Z_2$ invariant computed from a $\pm 1$ involution (parity) — supports the position that "$Z_2$ as a binary involution" is a well-developed CM language for the F-identity paper's $C_3 \times Z_2$ structure.
